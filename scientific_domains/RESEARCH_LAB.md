## Research use and admission

Research Lab may consume current-core APIs or frozen historical evidence when
explicitly allowed. Keep the selected API/artifact identity and historical
path/version/hash visible. A modern research implementation receives its own
identity and states any changed semantics; it must not impersonate a historical
execution or silently modify current-core authority.

Distinguish `EXPERIMENTAL_ENGINE` from `EMPIRICAL_FINITE_HISTORY`, exact conditional
results from `MODEL_CHOICE`, and documentary `PUBLICATION_EVIDENCE` from executed
science. Unresolved physical interfaces include shell attachment, boundary
calibration, geometry/dynamics coupling, physical units and observer-coordinate
meaning; the [Atlas boundary register](../research/mathematical_atlas/README.md#open-questions-and-interpretation-boundaries)
retains their specific qualifications.

Scientific admission requires separate review of claims, hypotheses, evidence
and authority. Public API support and UI exposure require their own contracts;
neither follows automatically from mathematical acceptance. Existing applications
may display experimental results with their provenance, but display cannot make
an experiment current accepted mathematics. Application architecture work and
all new research remain outside this navigation change.
