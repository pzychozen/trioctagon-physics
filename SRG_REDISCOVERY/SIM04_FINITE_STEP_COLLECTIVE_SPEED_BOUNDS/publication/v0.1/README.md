# Finite-Step Collective-Speed Bounds in Early SRG Motion I

## Component Profiles, Bound Dominance, and Remaining Slack

A vector bound can be valid yet far above the difference of collective speeds. This paper explains the finite-step hierarchy, why absolute-component profiles improve it, and the remaining norm-projection and center-cancellation losses. The weighted profile-dominance theorem is explicitly attributed to the subsequent GPT SIM04 review. The absent sufficient nonincrease passes are kept separate from the actual saved decreases and increases.

Both papers analyze the same saved repeated-driving population: 1,000 distinct updates, including the inherited prefix once. They are not two independent trajectory experiments. Bounds condition on realized sequential intermediates or profiles; no exact damped readout was evaluated on saved records. The real-valued inequalities were checked with float64 tolerances, not formal outward-rounded arithmetic.
