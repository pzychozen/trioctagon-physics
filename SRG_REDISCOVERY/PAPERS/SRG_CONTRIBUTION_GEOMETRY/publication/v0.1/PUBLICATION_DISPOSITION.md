It proves raw-prefix permanence; the legal integer-carry balance condition; the distinction between isolated carries, full carry cascades and reciprocal scaling; the exact scale-plus-reciprocal neighboring-pair law; and positive-block telescoping.

It also proves that the full raw contribution w3=0.508 is the unique maximum over all depths, using a certified finite prefix and an all-depth tail bound. The separate canonical-positive-adjacent imbalance bound is 1/19. The anchor's finite rank 1 of 62 and all other declared scan facts remain explicitly finite; they do not settle the infinite nearest-relative-balance question.

General positive-pair identities are not claimed to be unique to SRG or globally novel.
