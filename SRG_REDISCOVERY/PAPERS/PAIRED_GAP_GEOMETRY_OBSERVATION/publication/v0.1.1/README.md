# Paired-Gap Geometry and Toroidal Observation Maps for the Folded Tri-Octagon

**Hilmir Frímann Halldórsson — Paper I, v0.1.1.** Manuscript dated 18 October 2026.
Author-authorized repository research edition; internally reviewed, not externally peer reviewed.

[Read the 25-page PDF](PAPER_I_PAIRED_GAP_GEOMETRY_OBSERVATION_v0.1.1.pdf) · [Editable manuscript](manuscript.tex) · [Build guide](BUILD.md) · [Source and access map](ACCESS_AND_EVIDENCE_MAP.md) · [Publication disposition](PUBLICATION_DISPOSITION.md).

The paper derives a conditional normal-crossing circle at paired finite measurement regions of the unchanged folded shell; complete quarter-turned reference modules; exact reconstruction and information limits for a uniform observation map; and a separately adopted delay-only comparator. Full proofs include geometric clearance, a rank-four native observation result with exceptional strata, height/radius distinctions and a contact-boundary counterexample. The geometry and observation laws are not asserted to supply a physical energy-exchange mechanism.
