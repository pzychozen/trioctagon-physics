This edition does not claim that every historical report or private design note is publicly accessible. The full proof text and selected numerical/coordinate inputs are included; private work orders and author conversations are not a substitute for those proofs.

Exact asset hashes and source roles are recorded in the source-asset index. Private archive-member locators and the source/claim review ledgers remain in the owner's publication evidence, rather than being copied wholesale into this edition.
