# Claim-to-evidence matrix

This is an editorial traceability index, not a new scientific verification run. Every measured result below is retained from an accepted study, with its original coverage. The manuscript places claim IDs next to the associated discussion. Units are source coordinate/model-step units unless expressly labelled movie seconds.

- S01/S02/S03: byte-identical [source copies](MANUSCRIPT/sources); exact original locations/hashes in the manuscript Appendix A.
