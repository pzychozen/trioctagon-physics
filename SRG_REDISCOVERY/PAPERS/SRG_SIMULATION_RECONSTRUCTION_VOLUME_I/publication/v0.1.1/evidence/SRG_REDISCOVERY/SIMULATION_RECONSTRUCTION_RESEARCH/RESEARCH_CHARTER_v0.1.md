## Mission

Recover, understand and document **every distinct surviving historical SRG simulation** to the degree its original sources and evidence allow. Start with the complete experimental construction rather than a preferred equation or a remembered outcome. Preserve the origins, exact mechanisms, visible behavior, recorded data, changes across versions, and mathematical consequences. Ultimately publish a carefully reviewed, source-linked **SRG Simulation Reconstruction Research** work on the `trioctagon-physics` GitHub repository.

This is an evidence-led historical and mathematical reconstruction, **not** a demand that every historical simulation prove gravity, path inheritance, consciousness, or a theory of everything. Such interpretations may be considered only after the original mechanism is established.

## How to process each historical simulation

Complete and review **one** simulation before starting the next. Use the following sequence for every independently identified runnable simulation or clearly distinct behavior-bearing version:

1. **Identify the original:** Exact file path, hash, historical aliases, known date claims, inputs, dependencies, and the closest related version. Record uncertainty; directory names alone are not chronology.
2. **Understand construction:** What entities exist? Which variables persist? How are entities initialized? What order do calculations run in? How do interactions, time labels, damping, random choices, geometry and display actually work? Read the **whole program**, including callbacks and output writes.
3. **Recover the original evidence:** Inspect attributable movies, figures, CSVs, logs and notebooks. Preserve raw bytes. Verify attribution using multiple indicators, not filenames alone. Review video frames and recorded numerical trajectories **before** inventing replacement data.
4. **Reconstruct the experiment's execution history, where possible:** Distinguish numerical update count from animation frame, wall-clock time, plotting sample, and callback invocation. Preserve row order and repeated frame keys. Mark ambiguous provenance; never manufacture lost random state.
5. **Explain what actually occurs:** Use observed data or certified calculations to describe convergence, divergence, rotation, wrapping, switching, near-contacts, apparent jumps, and collective responses. Separate scene artifacts from state changes and describe where each interpretation is or is not supported.
6. **Connect the mechanism to mathematics:** Transcribe exact original equations after reviewing source execution order. Establish source-derived identities, analytically proved properties, measured behavior, and open interpretations under separate labels. Never retrofit Newtonian gravity, new forces or missing memory structures.
7. **Compare to nearby versions:** Identify precisely what was added, removed or altered, why that may matter mathematically, and which behavioral conclusions remain untested. Do not assume similarity means identical dynamics.
8. **Human review checkpoint:** GPT verifies findings and discusses them with Hilmir in ordinary language. Preserve the package and update the cumulative ledger. Only then choose the next simulation.

### Reconstruction evidence classes

- **ORIGINAL:** Byte-preserved historical program, image, movie, CSV, notes, or contemporaneous record.
- **ATTRIBUTED:** Historical artifact with corroborated but not definitive link to a specific source/run.
- **REPRODUCED:** New run of unchanged rules with recorded environment and initial state; never mislabeled as the lost original.
- **DERIVED:** Mathematical conclusion following from explicit equations and conditions.
- **OBSERVED:** Quantified or directly inspected behavior in original or reproduced evidence, with that distinction stated.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Plausible interpretation or memory-based research lead not established by the above.
- **MISSING / AMBIGUOUS:** Evidence or source detail not recovered; no silent reconstruction.

## Cumulative research infrastructure

Build on the **already existing** `R0_SOURCE_RECOVERY`, `FOUNDATIONS_01`, equation/operator lineage and the completed simulation studies; do not restart a repository-wide historical audit. Use them as a provisional index, then resolve one simulation family at a time.

Maintain a cumulative `SIMULATION_STUDY_LEDGER.md` under a new, dedicated research workspace within `SRG_REDISCOVERY`. Minimum columns/fields for each entry:

- Stable study ID; original source path and SHA-256; any confirmed aliases.
- Original media/data paths with hash, attribution and format.
- Historical date/version evidence and its limitations.
- State dimension, initialization, randomness, interaction structure, update order, clock(s), memory/history, boundary geometry, rendering, logging and output.
- Main observed behavior with precise record/frame/row anchors.
- What is known mathematically, what is only suggested, and key unresolved issues.
- Completed study package location; GPT/Hilmir review status; dependencies and relation to adjacent programs.

The ledger is a **navigation map**, not a demand to summarize all sources in advance. Distinct historical versions must remain distinct. Identical-byte duplicates can share one technical analysis while keeping their original provenance entries.

## High-detail study package standard

For each simulation, request a compact front door and comprehensive evidence underneath:

- `START_HERE.md`: friendly, accurate explanation of construction and the most interesting observed behavior; honest limits.
- `CONSTRUCTION.md`: full operation-by-operation walkthrough, state dependencies, mathematical transcription, exact source locators.
- `ORIGINAL_EVIDENCE.md`: historical media/data provenance, inspected scenes, row chronology and analysis limits.
- `BEHAVIOR_ANALYSIS.md`: measured/observed movement versus derived mechanism; figures with full labels; never cherry-pick unexplained screenshots.
- `COMPARISON_AND_LINEAGE.md`: differences from prior study and plausible development links, with uncertainty.
- `VERIFICATION_AND_PRESERVATION.md`: reproducible independent checks, original hashes, unchanged Git/source state, limitations.
- `MANIFEST.json`, source copies and attributable original evidence; small read-only inspection scripts and any **approved** replay materials.

Study packages may add relevant material (e.g. explicit randomness, source callback trace, energy-accounting appendix). File names can adapt to actual evidence; the scientific content must not be omitted for convenience.

### Experiments and preservation

Inspect existing evidence first. If original records are insufficient, GPT and Hilmir can authorize **one** bounded, faithful replay at a time. Record software/environment, seed or exact initializer, every relevant event, and reproducibility status. Never overwrite originals or imply a fresh run is historical. No parameter sweeps, seed hunting or rewritten dynamics without a distinct approved investigation.

Do not edit `kernel_physics`, historical sources, production TORMENT or already accepted papers. Avoid branches or cleanup unrelated to the active task. Keep all new SRG research inside the authoritative `SRG_REDISCOVERY` tree. Do not stage, commit, push or publish as part of an ordinary study checkpoint.

## Final research publication — after enough complete studies

**Working title:** *SRG Simulation Reconstruction: Historical Construction, Dynamics, and Emergent Geometry*.

The final work should be high-detail and reconstructive, not merely a list of screenshots or a retrospective narrative. Candidate organization:

1. Original development and source-provenance methods.
2. Simulation-by-simulation experiments (methods, source rules, state and output, measured behavior).
3. Exact comparison of attraction mechanisms: pairwise, centroid, switching/thresholds, geometric neighbor/weight/amplitude coupling, echo/memory and later operator models, only as supported by the individual studies.
4. Time, recursion and update clocks as actually implemented, with the previously flagged conceptual time theories investigated **separately**.
5. Mathematically established relationships and remaining unresolved hypotheses.
6. Distinctions among original observations, new reproductions, derived results and physical interpretations.
7. Full source/data/media evidence catalogue, hashes, reproducibility recipes, limitations and provenance ledger.

Possibly multiple volumes/chapters if breadth warrants. Do not collapse historically different systems into one universal recurrence. When each substantial chapter is solid, GPT and Hilmir review the scientific claims and final bytes. **GitHub publication is a separate approved step:** choose exact paths, preserve prior editions, verify every published file locally and remotely, and only then close out the publication. Do not claim repository publication before it has occurred.
