\section{Excluded and qualified evidence}
The curvature-labelled CSV previously indexed by R0 belongs to a
different, incompletely recovered rule. It is not evidence for
S02 or S03's trajectories here. The nearby CRE/DRE and harmonic-tunnel
images inspected in Study 03 contain quantities or narratives absent
from the selected program; they are not used as behavior evidence.
No neighboring movie or memory-based description substitutes for
an unrecovered run.

The chart-axis and detector findings qualify how historical outputs
should be read; they do not alter those outputs. The wording
“delayed direction reversal” in this manuscript refers to the
recorded velocity relative to a source-defined centroid direction,
without claiming physical calibration. The signed response mode
is likewise not an invariant label for attraction whenever H
changes sign.
