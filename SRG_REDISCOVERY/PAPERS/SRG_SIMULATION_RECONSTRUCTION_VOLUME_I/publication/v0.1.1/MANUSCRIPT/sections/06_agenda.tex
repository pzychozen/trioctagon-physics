\chapter{Open questions and the next volumes}\label{ch:agenda}
\plain{The first volume gives us a reliable account of three constructions.
It also makes the missing pieces more precise. Future work should begin
with a source or record that can answer a stated question, rather than
trying to reproduce a remembered picture. No further experiment is
started or authorized by this chapter.}

\section{Recoverable identities and execution histories}
The highest-priority provenance questions concern the run behind
S01's historical PNG, the numerical history behind S02's movie,
and a source/RNG/export chain for S03's movie and logs.
An export receipt, saved initializer, original environment record
or ordered trajectory could change the level of attribution.
Without such evidence, image similarity remains corroboration
rather than a certified historical join. \claim{C93}

S03's observed repeated labels already establish that numerical
updates and encoded frames are different counts. The missing
historical GUI event trace leaves the cause of each pass unresolved.
For S02, even the full callback count is missing. A future study
could examine historical environment records or a separately
approved bounded reproduction of callback behavior, but this
volume performs neither. The current Matplotlib excerpts document
a concrete compatible implementation, not a recovered historic
software version. \claim{C94}

\section{Original time concepts and equation lineage}
The source-defined time arguments are now explicit: a direct loop
index in S01 and supplied animation labels in S02/S03.
The broader conceptual origin of H, the intended interpretation
of the names REFU/TGMO/RPCO/GRF, and their relationships to other
SRG time equations require a separate lineage investigation.
The implemented functions alone cannot settle their historical
motivation or a physical theory of time. Study order and directory
numbering are not substitutes for dated source evidence.
\claim{C95}

\section{Path inheritance as a question about a chronology}
The remembered chronology in which one component travels first
and a later close pair follows an earlier route remains a research
question. It requires identities, ordering, a specified finite
window and a distinction between approximate following and exact
coincidence. An accumulated static line picture supplies too
little of that information. The seed-0 near-contact does not
establish the remembered episode. \claim{C96}

The earlier compatibility and initializer investigations are
separate limited mathematical analyses of specified branches and
conditions, as retained by the research charter. They are not
recovered historical trajectories, a universal no-go theorem for
SRG, or a reason to reject approximate collective behavior when
exact coincidence fails. This volume neither extends those proofs
nor tests another initializer. \cite{charter,volumeorder}
\claim{C97}

\section{Order, wrapping and attribution of causes}
The source reconstruction identifies order dependence,
arithmetic wrapped-coordinate averaging and display conventions
as mechanisms that can alter interpretation. Separately approved
future work could quantify their effects in a controlled
comparison. Such a comparison would have to distinguish the
historical rule from an explicitly modified diagnostic model,
retain a known initializer and clock, and avoid presenting
alternative rules as restoration of lost source.
\claim{C98}

For S03, a full audit of every velocity transition would increase
verification coverage beyond the accepted sample, but is not
performed here. Recovering the two body 7 chart generators would
also resolve their exact event-selection and horizontal-axis
conventions. The old charts are retained with a qualification,
not silently corrected or replaced. \claim{C99}

\section{Later families and the gravity question}
Later geometric, amplitude-coupled and neighbor-change simulation
families remain outside Volume I. They should be reconstructed
one source at a time using their own state, interactions, evidence
and execution history. Fixed-grid models, glyph/echo narratives,
March engines and production kernels must not be combined into
one universal recurrence merely because terms recur.
\cite{charter} \claim{C100}

A future comparison between attractor response, geometry and
gravity-related energy quantities may be scientifically useful.
It begins as a question about exact existing equations and
their domains, not as a result of the present kinetic diagnostic.
Nothing here identifies the coordinate measuring framework as a
physical container or force, adds a missing gravitational law,
or makes a physical conservation claim from the movie.
\claim{C101}

\section{The review boundary}
Volume I is a substantial first reconstruction of three selected
programs, not completion of the SRG project. Scientific and
editorial review is complete. Subsequent experiments and
GitHub publication remain separate decisions. This edition
preserves the accepted science. Publication preparation does
not itself authorize staging, commits, pushes, releases or
another experiment. \claim{C102}
