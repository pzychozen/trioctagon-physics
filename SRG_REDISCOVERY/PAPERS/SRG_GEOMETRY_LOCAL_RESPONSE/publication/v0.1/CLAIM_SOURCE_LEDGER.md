## Mathematical and editorial boundaries

All central local theorems have proofs in the manuscript. No hypothesis is supplied only by this ledger. The complete enhanced-addition formula is in Appendix A and its switch contribution is explicit in the main theorem.

The notation T_G for January's stencil operator replaces the report's T only to distinguish it from the Tower II source key. This changes no operator. The two models retain different carriers, constants and claims.

No substantive mathematical correction to the accepted reports was required by the bounded manuscript cross-check. Citation typography and exposition were adjusted in the new draft. Private QA records identify the actual artifact checks; they do not claim another mathematical test suite.
