## Scientific boundary

The chapter establishes conditional local amplitude response inside a fixed selected-neighbor region, an isolated fifth/sixth-neighbor exchange law retaining the selected enhanced-addition term, and the response of the existing signed compressor. The fixed scaled seven-particle family has equal output amplitudes throughout its stated interval, despite a nonzero precompression jump.

These are comparisons of incoming configurations at one common pass, not generated trajectories. Equality of amplitudes is not a theorem of full moving-particle-state capture or later identical evolution. January's fixed periodic field, signed mode, constants and one-step capture statements remain a separate reference. Its persistence conditions are not transferred to Tower II.

The chapter does not rename or complete the reserved SRG-II mechanism paper. SRG-I, SRG Origins, historical sources and accepted OC03/OC04 records remain unchanged.
