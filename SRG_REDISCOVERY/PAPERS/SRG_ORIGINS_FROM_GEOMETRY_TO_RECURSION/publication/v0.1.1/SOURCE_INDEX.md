The January paper cover title differs from the title of Zenodo record 18285147. The three selected program files and the paper in that deposit match the accepted January source bytes. Record 18366687 contains matching tgmo.py, refu.py and paper, but a different app.py; this edition cites 18285147 for the exact four-file set.

Public access checks, HTTP outcomes and exact downloaded-byte comparisons are retained in the private publication review records. A linked deposit is access to a source, not permission to apply its notice to this new manuscript.
