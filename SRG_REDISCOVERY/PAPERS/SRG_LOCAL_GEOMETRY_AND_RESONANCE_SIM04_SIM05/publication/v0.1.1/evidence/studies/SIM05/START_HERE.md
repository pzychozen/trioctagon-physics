# SIM05 — Infinity, recovered and replayed

Hilmir, this program moves 50 points through three-dimensional coordinates. Each point carries two ordinary numbers, R and D. Nearby points mix those numbers and move closer together. A second movement term pulls every point toward one shared, weighted center. Small random position changes continue on every pass. The stored “velocity” describes the last move; it does not push the next one.

Infinity keeps much of the previous Seed Explorer construction and adds a second real field. Its `.08699` coefficient actually enters the exchange between the two fields. The name “dark resonance” does not supply a physical identification with dark matter, and the two arrays are not a complex phase field.

The useful result is more specific than the original story of one uniform droplet. **The replay develops five compact local groups with different amplitude levels. Within each group, amplitudes become very similar. Across the whole field they remain different.** The historical CSV already disproves exact whole-field uniformity: its final maximum is 0.176329, mean 0.151203 and standard deviation 0.020442. The full five-group geometry is new replay evidence, not a recovered historical movie.

D stays positive and nonzero throughout this replay. It oscillates and develops different group levels. Its lower clipping bound activates three times; R never reaches either clipping bound. The field continues moving at pass 200, even though the direct H contribution has become tiny. Local movement, global fusion, cross-coupling, the R-only sine factor and positional noise remain in the equations.

The small SIM04 companion answers a separate question using its original saved coordinates. The six-point group both shrinks and changes relative shape. It retains spread in all three directions; neither six members nor its complete local graph establishes a named solid.

**Status: completed for GPT scientific review; SIM05 is not yet accepted or published.** The separate work order and the copy in the handoff were byte-identical. Exactly two isolated executions of the selected seed-2038 configuration used 200 passes each: **400 update calls total**, with no smoke runs. All states, metric outputs and complete RNG states match bit for bit between reference and capture. This supports attribution without certifying the lost original environment.
