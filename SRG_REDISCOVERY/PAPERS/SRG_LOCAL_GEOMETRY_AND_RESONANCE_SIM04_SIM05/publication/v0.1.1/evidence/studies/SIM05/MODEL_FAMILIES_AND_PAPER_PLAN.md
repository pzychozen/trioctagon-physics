Neither the numerical witness nor its checker was rerun. The fixed complex model and URF distinctions use R0's corrected Bridge/QSM and action discussion; no source map between these carriers and Infinity has been established.

SIM04 versus Infinity is **not a controlled one-factor comparison**. Both use seed2038 and start with the same X draw schedule, but the R interval changes, D consumes extra initialization draws, noise scales differ, H normalization differs, and observers have distinct timing. Any similarity of groups or difference in contraction cannot be attributed solely to adding D. A future controlled experiment would require separate authorization; none is proposed for automatic execution here.
