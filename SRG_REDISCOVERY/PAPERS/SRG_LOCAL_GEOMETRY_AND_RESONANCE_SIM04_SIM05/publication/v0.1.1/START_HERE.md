# For Hilmir: what these two simulations are doing

Each simulation keeps fifty distinct points. A point carries a position and a number called resonance; Infinity carries a second number as well. Every update rebuilds a local neighborhood, adjusts positions and exchanges those numbers, then applies scheduled changes, a pull toward one shared center, and positional noise. Points that overlap in a picture remain separate rows with their own identities.

The interesting result is that organization has several meanings. Seed Explorer's compact six-point group finishes with the **lowest** resonance values. Infinity develops five compact groups whose members almost agree in amplitude, while the groups have different amplitude levels and continue moving. A stable group or a high ranking does not mean that movement has stopped or that a point has become a causal leader.

This candidate turns the two accepted studies and both GPT reviews into one explanatory manuscript. It includes the SIM04 three-dimensional deformation addendum, the reviewed averaging and centroid arguments, and Infinity's exact endpoint echo/candidate interpretations and conditional uniformity proposition. It uses the existing evidence only.

## Principal conclusions

1. Seed Explorer's original 21 saved states show contraction and narrower amplitudes alongside local graph separation. Its six-point component shrinks **and deforms**, retaining spread in three covariance directions. Its point count and complete graph do not identify a named solid.
2. Infinity's accepted replay has five final groups of 11, 13, 10, 10 and 6 points. Membership persists from saved pass 33 through 200, with continuing motion and nearly equal amplitudes within each group. The historical aggregate CSV supports attribution and rejects whole-field equality; the complete grouping trajectory remains reproduced evidence.
3. All 238 endpoint echoes are precisely the internal unordered pairs in those five groups. The 44 candidates are exactly the points outside the low-amplitude six-point group. These conclusions use accepted pair-mask and candidate-mask checks, not just matching totals.
4. Four conditional mathematical statements explain the minimum isolated size, normalized amplitude smoothing, the mean fusion displacement and preservation of exact two-channel agreement within a locally closed group. None is a new physical law or an infinite-time convergence result.
