The [claim/source ledger](CLAIM_SOURCE_LEDGER.md) maps manuscript claims to the selected original files exposed under `sources/`. The accepted archives remain authoritative where historical relative paths inside those files refer to files not separately exposed. [EVIDENCE_IDENTITIES.json](EVIDENCE_IDENTITIES.json) records every copied file’s size and SHA-256.

The CA04 certificate and scalar consequences are exposed in `sources/CA04/`, together with the exact five-input index. Raw predecessor arrays and the standalone verifier remain in their accepted containers; displaying endpoint tables does not replace them. No scientific checker, forcing engine, derivative calculation, simulation or certificate replay was run for this edition.

The complete accepted archives are retained as scientific provenance, rather than repacked or edited. Local manuscript review ZIPs, start-packet instructions, draft baselines, caches, transient page images and publication working records are not part of this payload.
