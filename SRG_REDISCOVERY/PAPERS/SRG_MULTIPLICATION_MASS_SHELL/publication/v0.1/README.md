Start with two positive segments joined into a diameter. The semicircle's height above their junction is the square root of their product. The paper proves an exact inverse from the resulting future-hyperbola coordinates, explains how reciprocal rescaling acts, and identifies the information lost when four labelled factors are grouped into two. It then gives a conditional dimensional dictionary to the positive energy–momentum shell, without claiming an independently established SRG physical mass or propagation law.
