# Paper III: Compact Temporal Encodings

**Fourier Equivalence, Retrieval Obstructions and Saturation Effects**  
Hilmir Frímann Halldórsson · v0.1 · 19 October 2026

[Read the accepted 28-page paper](PAPER_III_COMPACT_TEMPORAL_ENCODINGS_v0.1.pdf).

The paper develops the exact completed-pulse representation and its invertible Fourier coordinates, timing-domain restrictions and aggregate information limits. It presents retained observation and cost measurements, derives the event-wise saturation mechanism, and retains its exact collisions, covered errors, prefix dependence and one-eighth-lag failure theorem.

The accepted manuscript and computed figures are preserved byte for byte. The original “Draft v0.1” title, headers and PDF metadata remain part of this unchanged edition; publication of this edition was authorized on 19 October 2026.
