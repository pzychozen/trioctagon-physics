# Tower II and Universe — candidate v0.1

Hilmir, this paper explains two ways the old programs made points move together. Tower II gives every permanently assigned band one common center. Universe, with a finite horizon, gives each point a center made from its selected neighbors. It works through the points in row order, so a later point can read a neighbor that has already moved during that same pass.

The saved records make those differences concrete. Tower II becomes tightly concentrated, but its points retain their identities and still move. Universe retains separated populations and eventually ten stable measured groups, while its neighbor connections and point positions continue changing. The paper distinguishes those measured groups from bands, input populations and graph components.

Start with [the manuscript](manuscript.pdf), Section 1 and its early figure. Sections 5, 7 and 9 explain the observed motion and original tables. Sections 3, 4, 6 and 10 give the exact operations and reviewed mathematics; Section 11 compares the programs. The two trajectories are not a controlled test of fusion alone.
