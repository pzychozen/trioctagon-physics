# SRG Final Mathematical Bridge

## From Lens Geometry to Recurrence and Known Equations

**Publication edition v0.1 - 27 October 2026**

Prepared for Hilmir Frímann Halldórsson. Accepted scientific and editorial synthesis; E01 closed and O01 accepted.

[Complete PDF](SRG_FINAL_MATHEMATICAL_BRIDGE_v0.1.pdf) · [Editable Markdown](SRG_FINAL_MATHEMATICAL_BRIDGE_v0.1.md) · [Equation/source appendix](EQUATION_SOURCE_APPENDIX.md)

The manuscript explains two established routes: lens geometry through adopted amplitude preparation and fixed SRG transfer to a separate nonlinear initialization, and periodic spectra through the standard quantum-ring comparison to a calibrated historical coupling. It connects the recurrences to familiar gradient, phase and oscillator equations while preserving their assumptions, clocks, raw amplitudes and zero cases. The historical coupling-selection question remains explicitly unresolved and does not block this publication.
