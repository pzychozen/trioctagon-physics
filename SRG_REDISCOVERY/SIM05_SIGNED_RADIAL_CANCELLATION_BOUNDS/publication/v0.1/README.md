# Finite-Step Collective-Speed Bounds in Early SRG Motion II

## Signed Radial Intervals and Collective Cancellation

Signed radial intervals retain the direction and uncertainty of center-level speed differences. This paper explains the fixed one- and four-subinterval construction and the gain from signed averaging of the same intervals. It preserves both decrease and increase conditions, nonzero uncertainty, the nonpassing new-four damping phases 17 and 57, and large adverse pair factors.

**Author:** Hilmir Frímann Halldórsson. **Version:** 0.1, 14 October 2026.

Both papers analyze the same saved repeated-driving population: 1,000 distinct updates, including the inherited prefix once. They are not two independent trajectory experiments. Bounds condition on realized sequential intermediates or profiles; no exact damped readout was evaluated on saved records. The real-valued inequalities were checked with float64 tolerances, not formal outward-rounded arithmetic.
