What this viewer measures

Every original straight line is m ± t u. R²=w²/3+t², C=2t, ell=t−(√2−1)w/2. C is the whole chord; ell is the added length at each end. These are three different quantities.

The first new meeting is A; the last finite meeting is B, on every outward ray. A has C=w; B has ell=w. They are incidence-based stopping conventions, not physical walls.

The split graph contains every actual spatial meeting inside the chosen event sphere. The convex envelope fills a separate surface. Gold lines show maximal-face hull edges, not rendering diagonals. At A and B, all open graph segments lie in the hull interior.

The green sphere is tangent to edges at their midpoints and cuts panel interiors. Exact coincidences are certified only by event presets or the exact length identities C/w=1, ell/w=0 or ell/w=1. Other numeric entries retain all 42 branch markers; visual overlap never certifies coincidence.
