# Method reference

G. Niemeyer and J.-J. E. Slotine. **Stable adaptive teleoperation.** *IEEE Journal of Oceanic Engineering* 16(1), 152–162 (1991). DOI: [10.1109/48.64895](https://doi.org/10.1109/48.64895). [Publisher record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/64895/).

The publisher's indexed abstract describes wave variables and passivity-based energy/stability accounting for delayed interactions. It was consulted on 10 November 2026 to ground the general methodology. The full paper is not copied into this packet and is not used as a black-box theorem for the proposed Tri-Octagon equations.

The specific J/C operators, boundary laws, mild-solution argument, receiving witness and C6 relation are derived in the analytical start and require independent review. No novelty is claimed for the general use of traveling wave variables to account for delayed exchange.
