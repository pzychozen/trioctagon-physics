## Essential distinctions in the prose and equations

1. **Two adjoints.** W's \(\mathcal K_G^*\) acts on a complete temporal observation window and samples a zero-extended test at \(u+\theta\). T's \(C^*\) is a same-time spatial adjoint used with stateful causal transport. The construction does not make the original temporal adjoint causal or contradict W03. Include the short-window case \(T\le\theta_-\), where its forward restriction vanishes a.e.
2. **Two prehistory concepts.** Preserve \(\Delta H_{\rm base}+W^{\rm test}-B_N=W_{\rm pre}\), with a positive coefficient on the right but a signed bilinear pairing. New \(E_{\rm tr}(0)\ge0\) is a quadratic stock of explicitly declared initial transport states. They are not identified. Zero prehistory is sufficient, not necessary, for \(W_{\rm pre}=0\).
3. **C6 inside the actual loaded response.** Print the complete startup identity, including the \(+\sqrt2Cc_{\rm init}\) term and coefficient \(2\kappa\) on the direct C6 component. Print the simpler complete-history formula only with negative-time near-law and forward-profile compatibility. Neither ζ nor its full drive equals bare C6 in general.
4. **D01.** Use exactly the logical scope: at κ=0 the old subsystem is RX for every new-state preparation; the isolated new subsystem may evolve from its stock; empty added states are sufficient for inactivity, not necessary. A nonzero normal-direction ζ in \(\ker C^*\), with empty lines, is a stationary positive-stock counterexample to necessity. Keep the historical work order unchanged and record the documentary clarification separately.
5. **Funding and return.** T07 starts from a declared carrier preload with native state zero. It is not native empty-launch receiving/recovery. Nonzero incoming b does not establish nonzero \(J^*b\), positive net carrier work, native credit or recovery. Immediate \(-\kappa^2Q\) launching remains present while the incoming return is delayed.
6. **Old results.** Unchanged native/old-z equation forms do not preserve their old coupled trajectories at κ>0. Earlier capacity, recovery, credit and convergence theorems are not inherited automatically. Q01, phase-information, cadence and singular-prestage qualifications remain at their existing scopes.

Use a notation crosswalk: native vectors a_N,b_N versus boundary a,b,c,d; old carrier speed c_car versus boundary c and observation speed c_e; native derivative J_f versus duplication J; original profile operator \(\mathcal B\) versus any Gram matrix; old receiver z versus new ζ; static geometry parameter τ_g versus time; full ball radius versus cylindrical radius; carrier arclength s versus channel time coordinate ξ.
