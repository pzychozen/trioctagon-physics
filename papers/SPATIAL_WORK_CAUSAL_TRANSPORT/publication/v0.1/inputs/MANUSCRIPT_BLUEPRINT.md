## Central argument

The inherited C6 operator already supplies a causal spatial observation of carrier history. Its exact finite-window adjoint has advanced dependence on sufficiently long windows, and its conditional work residual retains signed prehistory. One declared state extension supplies causal transport and receiving storage with exact matched work. Prove its evolution and activity at the accepted scope, then explain the remaining physical correspondence.

This argument includes both W and T. Neither the temporal-adjoint difficulty nor the new construction should be reduced to a slogan. The mathematical extension is not uniquely selected by W and is not an already calibrated physical mechanism.

## Logical proof order

The main theorem should follow the actual noncircular construction. First establish characteristic trace/continuity facts. Construct a local forced RX mild solution and the independent receiving ODE on each delay slab. Use the carrier interaction variable and original finite-dimensional chain rules to derive the RX input-energy inequality. Continue the slab using that inequality. Then construct outgoing transport and combine the component identities into total conservation. Iterate, and finish with uniqueness and finite-horizon dependence.

Keep energy-state regularity, boundary time traces, continuous observation records and temporary activity-witness H1 regularity separate. Positive storage need not imply monotonic receiver growth. Exchange conservation does not imply a backward-flow theorem.
