Abstract.   The  inherited  C6  formula  observes  a  periodic  carrier  at  six  central  broken
boundaries through retarded, distance-weighted histories.  We establish its bounded causal
action  on  a  complete  space-time  history  domain,  distinguish  that  estimate  from  point
records, and derive its ﬁnite-window temporal adjoint.  On suﬃciently long windows the
forward part of that adjoint is advanced;  a formal negative-adjoint force leaves a signed
prehistory  term  in  the  work  account.   We  then  declare  one  stateful  causal  extension  of
the reciprocal chiral exchange candidate.  Two traveling ﬁelds on static delay intervals and
a separate receiving ﬁeld have nonnegative quadratic stocks.  Spatial eﬀort/ﬂow coupling
yields  exact  matched  work  without  requiring  the  mixing  operator  to  be  contractive.   A
method-of-steps  argument  proves  unique  global  forward  mild  evolution  for  every  ﬁnite-
energy preparation, with locally square-integrable boundary signals and exact augmented
exchange conservation.  C6 remains the direct component of the loaded receiving equation,
whose  return,  loading  and  arbitrary-proﬁle  startup  terms  are  retained  explicitly.   The
original native map retains its ﬁnite-window baseline accounting formula.  Empty added
states  impose  positive  receiving  and  return  delays;  a  constant  carrier  preload  produces
strict receiving and a nonzero incoming return on speciﬁed short intervals.  This witness
is  carrier-funded  and  does  not  establish  native  recovery.   The  result  is  a  mathematical
transport/storage connection; its physical curved-route, material and clock correspondence
remains open.

1    Question and progression of the argument

The reciprocal chiral exchange candidate couples a ﬁnite native state to a periodic carrier and
an unplaced reversible receiver through ﬁnite distributed proﬁles.  Its exact work account estab-
lishes actual changes of trial stocks.  The preceding central-route and six-opening correspondence
study showed why those changes alone do not identify completed spatial travel:  receiving can
precede selected carrier activity, and the retained carrier is separated from all six central broken
boundaries and their individual convex enclosures at the common registration [4].  That sepa-
ration is compatible with observing the carrier from the boundaries.  It does not supply a load
there, and it does not assign zero to an undeﬁned physical aperture ﬂux.
The  inherited  C6  formula  provides  a  deﬁnite  retarded  observation.   It  integrates  two  carrier
components, encoded as a scalar and a tangent vector, with endpoint-distance delay and inverse-
distance weight.  The ﬁrst question is what that formula deﬁnes on the actual central arcs and
what its exact mathematical dual says about work.  The second is constructive:  can a declared
extension retain that observation as a direct component while supplying causal evolution, non-
negative intermediate storage and matched work?
We answer these questions in one progression.  First, the actual geometry supplies ﬁnite measures
and  a  complete  history  horizon.   A  space-time  estimate  gives  a  bounded  causal  observation
operator;  a  diﬀerent,  stronger  regularity  hypothesis  supplies  continuous  point  records.   The
ﬁnite-window temporal adjoint necessarily includes prehistory and, on suﬃciently long windows,
depends on future test values.  Its direct use as a formal carrier force therefore provides neither a
causal load nor an instantaneous power identity.  The associated integrated work identity retains
a signed prehistory pairing.
Next  we  declare  two  traveling  states  on  a  continuum  of  static  delay  intervals  and  a  separate
receiving ﬁeld indexed by the central arcs.  An isometric duplication and a bounded spatial mixer
factor C6 exactly. The mixer and its same-time adjoint enter eﬀort/ﬂow boundary relations; they
are not the temporal adjoint of the retarded operator.  Characteristics supply time traces and
a nonnegative transport stock.  A local input-energy estimate, obtained before assuming total
conservation, continues the coupled solution through each delay slab.  This proves global forward
mild evolution and exact augmented exchange conservation for every ﬁnite-energy preparation.
Finally,  the  full  receiving  equation  retains  C6,  return,  loading  and  startup  contributions;  the
original native jumps retain their baseline account; and a speciﬁed carrier preload gives strict
delayed receiving and an incoming-return witness.
The mathematical foundations are the accepted project-internal investigations W01–W05 and
T01–T07 [2,     3].  The present paper provides their principal derivations rather than treating the
acceptance records or their exact-algebra counts as proofs.  The two investigations have diﬀerent
roles:  W analyzes the inherited observation and a conditional work pairing, whereas T declares
additional states, metrics and boundary laws and proves their properties.  The construction is
one modeling choice, not a unique deduction from C6.
Wave-variable  and  passivity-based  treatment  of  delayed  interactions  is  established  methodol-
ogy [1].  That reference supplies context for the general method,  not a black-box theorem for
this inﬁnite-dimensional system.  The particular operators, arbitrary-proﬁle startup terms, non-
linear continuation argument and activity witness are established below.  No novelty is claimed
for the general use of traveling variables to account for delayed exchange.
The  intended  physical  picture  retains  stationary  supports,  a  central  meeting  region,  opposed
directions, outward spreading, a curved return and possible transfer elsewhere.  Local exchange
and a larger circuit are compatible aims.  This paper completes an abstract mathematical con-
nection while leaving that physical attachment explicit as subsequent work.

9    What the connection establishes, and what remains spatial

The observation and transport investigations answer complementary questions.  A delayed C6
readout is a bounded causal map on the correct history space.  Its temporal dual is determined
by  that  domain  and  the  ﬁnite  test  window,  not  by  a  physical  transmission  mechanism.   The
signed  prehistory  term  identiﬁes  the  part  of  the  work  pairing  that  cannot  be  charged  to  the
forward  carrier  interval.   By  contrast,  the  extension  declares  additional  traveling  ﬁelds  and  a
separate  receiving  state,  and  proves  their  evolution  and  work  account.   The  new  preparation
carries a nonnegative quadratic stock.  It is not W’s signed bilinear prehistory pairing in another
notation.
The  constructive  result  is  substantial  at  its  stated  level:  positive  stocks,  global  forward  mild
evolution, exact total exchange conservation, the full loaded C6 identity, and a receiving/return
