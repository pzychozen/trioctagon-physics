# Approved manuscript measurement refinement

Scientific excerpt from the supplied GM01–GM03 manuscript order. The complete order is preserved locally; SHA-256 40a78da1da50164c037746c877e68c0bbe7e6663fbde1879af55e258ad712729. The excerpt below is unchanged.

Use “full registered state for the full vector test” wherever the one-page summary suggests that every scalar advance measurement needs all six coordinates. In this specified Fourier preparation, the middle complex channel is \(\Omega_B(s)=\omega(1-s/20)\) with \(|\omega|=1\) and \(1-s/20>0\) throughout the certified box. Hence a single calibrated amplitude gives
\[
s_{\rm on}=20\bigl(1-|\Omega_{1,B}|\bigr).
\]
This inference uses the known initial preparation, normalized amplitude, channel label and first-step path. It does not test the other components or certify that the complete loaded displacement follows the predicted direction.

GM03 (20) also bounds the middle channel’s next native displacement away from zero. With the same phase registration for the two matched copies, one may therefore test the necessary component equality
\[
\Omega_{2,E,B}-\Omega_{1,B}
=(2s_{\rm on}-1)\bigl(\Omega_{2,C,B}-\Omega_{1,B}\bigr).
\]
Here \(E\) denotes the exposed copy and \(C\) its own neutral control, with identical reached native state. A calibrated amplitude at the first step and a registered complex displacement at the next step are different measurements. A failure in this component suffices to reject the vector prediction under the controls. Agreement in one component leaves the others to be checked. Verify these two direct consequences against GM03 (1), (4) and (20) as part of drafting.
