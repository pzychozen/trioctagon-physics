Abstract
The native Tri-Octagon state determines an intensity and a geometry-attached axial vec-
tor. We derive their sharp inequality and use them in an explicitly added mass–dipole dic-
tionary. Newtonian gravity and magnetostatics are premises of this extension. For disjoint
radial source profiles, harmonic averaging gives an exact two-body interaction. Native source
updates alone do not balance its change: the same internal update produces different gravi-
tational work at different separations and opposite magnetic work in parallel and transverse
placements.
A proposed nonnegative store changes the advance along each frozen native update direc-
tion. Its midpoint rule conserves the specified pair-plus-store energy exactly and has a unique
solution in stated nonzero load ranges. An initially dipole-silent Fourier preparation yields
certified spatial response and neutral-placement predictions. Most directly , after one loaded
step with gain 𝑠on, a verified switch to zero load predicts the next gain 2𝑠on − 1, relative to a
neutral copy at the same reached state. This test can reject the feedback without identifying
a physical carrier. In this preparation, one calibrated channel amplitude determines the first
gain; the full registered state is needed for the full vector test. We separate mathematical pre-
dictions, parameter identifiability and independent energy measurements. A physical store,
instrument, clock and switching procedure remain to be identified.
Status and provenance. The three underlying investigations have project-internal mathematical accep-
tance recorded in the retained review evidence. The author approved this combined manuscript for
repository publication; no external peer review or physical validation is claimed. The author’s geometric
programme and research direction are retained. GPT supplied analytical proposals and review; Codex
supplied mathematical checks, source verification and manuscript preparation. Earlier papers and evi-
dence remain unchanged.
