EXACT MATHEMATICS AND NUMERICAL SUPPORT
verification/verify.py completed successfully with Python 3.11.15, SymPy 1.14.0,
and mpmath 1.3.0. All 77 exact symbolic checks passed. They cover the rational
identities, exceptional C-extension, involutions and normal form, quotient maps,
large-L chart identities, elliptic models/invariants, isogeny, and group actions.
The separate finite permutation check verifies transitivity, order 16, and the
identity product of the displayed branch cycles. These computational checks
support the manuscript's proofs; they do not replace the proofs or constitute a
machine-verified proof of the complete moduli/Hurwitz classification.

At 100 decimal digits, 48 nonsingular normal-form comparisons had maximum
normalized discrepancy 2.3146503400523145903e-100. The four historical examples
were evaluated at the same precision, including agreement of the three j-formulas.
The (pi,e) CM problem was not investigated further.

The six fixed intermediate-chart samples s = +/-0.5, +/-sqrt(2), +/-3 were checked
at L = 10^4, 10^8, 10^12. The largest profile errors were, respectively,
0.11323974956100712442,
0.0011003150872743067644,
0.000011000031500087250243.
Their ratios to L^(-1/2) approach approximately 11 on this finite sample. Separate
bulk and thin-lock overlap checks also converge; full values and definitions are
in verification/results.json and the manuscript's verification appendix. Finite
samples are numerical evidence only. The uniform compact-set convergence and
matching domains are proved algebraically in the paper.

LIMITATIONS AND STOP POINT
Printed historical dates were verified, but live Zenodo deposition timestamps
were not independently confirmed where retrieval failed. The old curvature and
stability pipelines were not rerun. The historical coefficient-label conflict is
documented rather than silently resolved. Author recollections are identified as
retrospective evidence. The (pi,e) CM status remains unresolved. The chain match
remains a reduced spectral-quotient identification, with no independently selected
full physical observable or additional x-cover meaning established.

Known literature was checked for attribution and mathematical terminology; a
broad novelty/priority search was deliberately not performed. The manuscript,
claim ledger, and exact audit-claim list are now ready for that separate stage.
