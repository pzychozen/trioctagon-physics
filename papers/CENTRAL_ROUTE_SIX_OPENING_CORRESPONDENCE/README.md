# Central-Route Accounting and Six-Opening Geometry in the Reciprocal Chiral Exchange Candidate

**Hilmir Frímann Halldórsson (pzychozen)** — research edition v0.1, 10 November 2026.

[Read the paper](publication/v0.1/CENTRAL_ROUTE_SIX_OPENING_CORRESPONDENCE_v0.1.pdf) · [Edition, editable source and evidence](publication/v0.1/README.md)

The paper combines exact regional work accounting with the six-opening geometry comparison. Under the stated empty-launch and nonzero-drive hypotheses, receiving can precede selected carrier travel events. The inherited carrier is uniformly separated from all six central boundaries and their individual convex enclosures by more than 0.0594 times common host width, throughout the admitted static family and both hands. Existing host contacts therefore cannot be renamed as direct central-opening crossings; a physical aperture flux remains undefined without an added transport/loading registration.

Accepted mathematics is preserved, including Q01. The actual manuscript review required M01–M03; the exact repairs and final document checks were completed under the author's subsequent publication authorization. See the edition's publication status and correction closeout for this sequence.
