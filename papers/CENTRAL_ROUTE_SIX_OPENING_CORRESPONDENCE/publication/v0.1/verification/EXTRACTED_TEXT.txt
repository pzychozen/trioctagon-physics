Abstract. The reciprocal chiral exchange candidate couples a native state to a spatially
registered periodic carrier and a reversible receiver through ﬁnite proﬁles. We examine
what its exact work account establishes about an intended outward–around–inward route.
For empty carrier and receiver preparation, positive couplings and a nonzero registered
drive, receiver storage grows at quartic order while the nearest carrier point to the central
origin remains inactive. Diagnostic cuts strictly enclosing both source proﬁles also remain
inactive initially. We derive the regional account, including complementary-proﬁle work
and native jumps, and give the full cut-distance criterion, which also permits delayed
inner cuts. Independently, a scalar separator proves that all three inherited geometric
circles are uniformly separated from each of the six central opening boundaries and its
individual convex enclosure, throughout the admitted static geometry family and for both
hands. The distance exceeds 297/5000 times the common host width. These enclosures are
mathematical bounds, not adopted apertures. Thus the existing host contacts cannot be
relabeled as direct central-opening crossings; an undeﬁned physical aperture ﬂux has not
been computed as zero. Common-phase symmetry further limits what exchange histories
alone can recover about a phase-sensitive outlet. The paper identiﬁes the additional spatial
and work registration needed to relate the demonstrated exchange to a larger transport
circuit.

1 Question and principal results
The geometric motivation is a central starting or meeting region, opposed upward and downward
routes, outward spreading and a curved return, possibly with transfer elsewhere, while the native
supports remain stationary. A correspondence argument must connect that picture to the actual
state variables and exchange laws. Storage gained by a receiver is an energetic statement;
crossing a speciﬁed spatial boundary is a separate geometric and dynamical statement.
The inherited candidate already supplies substantial structure: positioned native support lines,
a registered carrier curve with two ﬁnite host contacts, reciprocal native feedback, a reversible
receiver and a matched work account [ 2, 4, 5, 6]. The present paper asks how far those structures
realize the intended route. It combines two accepted internal investigations [ 8, 9] into one
argument at the unchanged registration.
The ﬁrst result is a temporal distinction. From empty carrier and receiver preparation with
nonzero registered drive, positive receiving occurs before activity at the carrier point nearest
the central origin. A related conclusion holds at diagnostic cuts with a positive distance from
the complete source-support union. The regional account explains why this does not violate
conservation: the receiver couples to the same distributed interfaces as the native drive, and
complementary work must be retained whenever the diagnostic region omits part of those inter-
faces.
The second result is spatial. For every admitted static geometry parameter and both hands, every
inherited circle lies at distance greater than 0.0594whost from each central opening boundary and
its individual convex hull, where whost is the common host width. This is an exact uniform lower
bound, not a sampled intersection test. Only one of the three geometric circles is instantiated
dynamically. The result excludes direct crossings by that unchanged carrier, while leaving room
for an explicitly added spatial connection.
Sections 2–4 establish the model, radial account and receiving comparison. Section 5 proves
the uniform separation. Section 6 combines the geometric result with direction and information
limits. Throughout, RB denotes a diagnostic ball radius , whereas r⊥(P ) denotes a cylindrical
radius. Appendix B records this and the other editorial symbol changes explicitly.

7 Synthesis and the completed correspondence boundary
The two results answer complementary questions. The temporal result shows that a receiver can
gain storage through the registered local interfaces before the selected carrier travel events. The
spatial result shows that those same interfaces remain separated from the six central boundaries
and their individual convex enclosures. Both statements coexist with exact reciprocal work,
radial approach and recession along the carrier, and the positioned stationary-axis dictionary.
To turn the intended route into a further transport model, a spatial registration must supply
three connected ingredients:
1. the actual set receiving or being crossed, together with orientation and measure when a
surface ﬂux is intended;
2. a map from the existing state, and any explicitly introduced additional state, to the trans-
ported or loaded quantity on that set;
3. the matching work and stock relation, so that receiving and the associated carrier/native
debit are consistently accounted for.
A measurement-only construction instead needs a declared observation interpretation and should
not be called an energetic load. These requirements identify the missing connection without
choosing a new aperture closure, connector, receiver, thickness, clock or eﬃciency target.
The candidate already has reciprocal feedback and a reversible receiver. Same-solution native
credit, exchange-only capacity-sized receiving/recovery, and the scoped cadence results are com-
pleted parts of the inherited programme [ 5, 6, 7]. Historical source sentences describing these
as absent or open do not control the present status. Later receiving/recovery after native steps
and preload remains a separate question; it is not supplied by the empty-launch theorem here.
Local exchange can participate in a later larger circuit, including transfer elsewhere. The present
results do not force a choice between those possibilities. They complete the correspondence
assessment at the retained deﬁnitions: receiving alone does not certify the speciﬁed travel, and
central-opening transport cannot be obtained by renaming the existing host contacts. Further
spatial connection is subsequent research, with no new strict order imposed on the other retained
directions.
The mathematical basis is the accepted C01–C05 investigation with Q01 and the accepted S01–
S05 investigation [ 8, 9]. The central-route record contains eight independently written algebra
groups with the more limited corroboration described by its GPT acceptance. The six-opening
record contains six independent exact-check groups and a later GPT replay of those same six
groups. The replay is not six additional independent proofs, and it is not a full replay of
the central-route suite. The arguments above supply the support, trace, convexity, Lipschitz
and interpretation steps for which arithmetic checks alone would not suﬃce. This manuscript
preparation uses the retained evidence, without a new scientiﬁc-suite execution or trajectory
simulation. Its claim ledger and document build/inspection records accompany the editable
edition; manuscript review remains separate from acceptance of its mathematical foundations.
