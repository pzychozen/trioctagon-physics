\begin{abstract}
We study one explicit candidate extension coupling the Tri-Octagon native state to its conditional periodic carrier and to a reversible two-port receiver. The existing raw chiral drive is retained, while a homogeneous reciprocal completion matches carrier work to changes in a positive trial native storage. A quadratic receiver storage has an equal and opposite carrier loading term. We prove global forward and backward exchange-time well-posedness in the natural energy space, with a separate regularity estimate for periodic $H^1$ preparations. Empty-carrier preparations exhibit nonzero short-time native supply and receiver gain; other prepared carrier states yield native credit. The exchange conserves the complex bilinear quantity $\sum_i\Omega_i^2$, imposing a restrictive bound on net extraction from coherent initial states and leaving aligned empty preparations dark. Explicit counterexamples prevent transfer of universal coherent-domain invariance to the coupled model. An ordered hybrid step retains the unchanged native map before exact exchange; its total storage increment is precisely the native baseline intensity increment, and zero coupling recovers the original native sampling. These are results about a mathematically verified candidate, not adoption into the accepted kernel or a derived physical energy law. Delayed native credit on the same solution after an empty launch, irreversible disposal and physical toroidal registration remain open.
\end{abstract}
