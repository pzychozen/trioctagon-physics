# Scientific blueprint for the combined paper

## Central argument

The intended geometric picture has a central starting/meeting region, opposed upward/downward routes, outward spreading, curved return and possible transfer elsewhere, with stationary supports. The present paper asks what the existing reciprocal candidate actually establishes about that picture.

There are two main results. First, its local reversible receiver can gain storage before the specified carrier travel events, so receiving does not by itself establish a completed spatial circuit. Second, its inherited registered centerline is uniformly separated from the actual central G boundaries and their individual convex hulls, so the present host N contacts cannot be renamed as central G crossings.

The positive foundations matter: exact positioned support lines, a real spatial carrier embedding, genuine reciprocal debit/credit and receiving, regional cut powers, and a source-linked comparison of the six openings. The paper should make those connections clear before identifying the missing transport registration.
