## 4. Why the principal inference is valid

Let S be the union of the two closed tent supports, and put s0 = Lc/2. Their centers lie at s0 ± ηg and their half-width is εc, with 0 < εc < ηg. For empty carrier preparation the actual coupled solution has characteristics

\[
w_\pm(s,t)=\frac1{\sqrt2}\int_0^t
[Bq(\tau)]\bigl(s\mp c(t-\tau)\bigr)\,d\tau,
\qquad q=\eta f(x)-\kappa z.
\]

Global port feedback changes q, but all forcing remains supported in S. Therefore a point at positive shortest-circle distance d from S has zero carrier field for 0 ≤ t < d/c. At s0 this distance is ηg − εc. Under strict enclosing-profile containment, each diagnostic cut instead has distance Aρ − ηg − εc.

The accepted launch expansion is

\[
H_R(t)=\frac{\eta^2\kappa^2}{8\beta}
\|M_Bf_0\|^2t^4+o(t^4),\qquad M_B=mI_2,
\]

with positive leading coefficient whenever f0 ≠ 0 and both couplings are positive. A sufficiently small common interval therefore has positive receiver stock and the stated zero carrier fields.

This is a statement about distance from the **union of both source supports**. Nonlocal feedback can change source coefficients at both arcs without waiting for an inter-port wave; no finite-speed theorem for arbitrary influence in the complete coupled system is inferred. A guaranteed initial zero-field interval also does not guarantee nonzero first arrival at its endpoint.

The local account independently confirms the interpretation. With Eρ the carrier stock on an intermediate-radius inner arc and P∂ its signed outward cut power,

\[
\Delta(H_N+H_R+E_\rho)
=B_N-\int P_\partial\,dt
-\int\!\int_{I_\rho^c}pBq\,ds\,dt.
\]

BN is zero for exchange-only windows and is the correctly counted native-jump sum for finite hybrid windows. Complete profile containment removes the last term. It does not give the lumped native and receiver stocks material positions.

## 5. Q01 — clarification of the review's necessity wording

The actual proposed C04 claim is a sufficient-condition statement and is correct. A later explanatory sentence in the Codex proof says that below containment there is no generic positive cut gap supplied by the argument. The corrections note also calls strict containment necessary for “this” positive cut delay. These passages should be understood specifically as statements about the **enclosing-support clearance** Aρ − ηg − εc, rather than as a necessity claim for every possible zero-cut interval.

A smaller diagnostic region entirely inside the central gap also has a positive support distance. For an intermediate inner half-arc A, the exact distance of either cut to S is

\[
d(A,S)=
\begin{cases}
\eta_g-\epsilon_c-A,&0<A<\eta_g-\epsilon_c,\\
0,&\eta_g-\epsilon_c\le A\le\eta_g+\epsilon_c,\\
A-\eta_g-\epsilon_c,&\eta_g+\epsilon_c<A<L_c/2.
\end{cases}
\]

This follows directly from the symmetric support intervals. In the outer case the alternative seam path is longer by Lc − 2A > 0; in the inner case the adjacent inner support endpoint is closest. Equivalently the distance is max(|A − ηg| − εc, 0).

For an exact witness, set εc = ηg/4 and A = ηg/2. These are admissible diagnostic parameters, and A < ηg − εc. Each cut has distance ηg/4 from S, so the same characteristic argument gives zero cut fields and powers for 0 ≤ t < ηg/(4c), despite failure of enclosing-profile containment. No working geometry or parameter choice is adopted by this witness.

The clarification to carry forward is:

> Strict containment gives the positive clearance for an enclosing region. When containment fails, that enclosing-region formula gives no positive bound; cuts lying in the inner source-free gap can still have a positive delay.

At a source-support endpoint the distance is zero. The Codex review's more specific equality calculation is correct: an initially active port produces an outgoing characteristic of order t² and outward power of order t⁴. Absence of a positive support gap elsewhere is not a general theorem that every field or flux must immediately be nonzero; preparation, symmetry and cancellation retain their roles.

This clarification narrows explanatory wording. It changes none of C01–C05, the width ceiling, the regional account, or the accepted receiving-before-activity conclusion.
