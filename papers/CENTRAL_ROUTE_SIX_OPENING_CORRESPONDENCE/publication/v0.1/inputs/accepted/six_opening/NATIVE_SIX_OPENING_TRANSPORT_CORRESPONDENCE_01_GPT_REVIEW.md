## 4. Why the geometric argument holds

The actual G vertices and the carrier share the same center O and common width. The lower horizontal map satisfies
\[
A_\tau^TA_\tau=\left(1+\tau^2/r_g^2\right)I,
\]
so its dilation is uniformly bounded by \(D_*=\sqrt{10-6\sqrt2}\). The prescribed MINUS reflection preserves cylindrical radius and absolute centered height.

For
\[
\Phi(P)=\rho(P)-\frac35|\zeta(P)|,
\]
each individual hull stays entirely in one height halfspace. On that halfspace \(\Phi\) is a convex norm minus a linear function. The strict bounds at its elbow and tips therefore extend to every hull point, giving \(\Phi\leq3/50\). The proof explicitly avoids a hull mixing upper and lower sets, for which this convexity argument would fail.

On each inherited circle, \(K_c>R_c\) removes the absolute-value ambiguity in cylindrical radius. Cauchy–Schwarz gives
\[
\Phi(\Gamma_j)\geq K_c-L_*R_c,\qquad L_*=\sqrt{34}/5.
\]
The exact rational estimates then give the uniform gap
\[
\Delta_*:=K_c-L_*R_c-\frac3{50}
>\frac{69321}{1000000}>0.
\]

This is initially a gap in the scalar function \(\Phi\). The submitted proof correctly establishes that \(\Phi\) is globally \(L_*\)-Lipschitz, including at the axis and across height signs. Hence
\[
\operatorname{dist}(\Gamma_j,\mathcal H_k^\sigma)
\geq\frac{\Delta_*}{L_*}
>\frac{23107}{389000}>\frac{297}{5000}.
\]
The exact uniform lower bound is retained before taking infima, so the strict comparison is justified. All coordinates and the static parameter scale together in the width-w statement. Independent repositioning or rescaling of one object is outside the theorem.

The exact conservative expression is approximately 0.06097180165653555 at width one. Its rounded display is consistent with the fine rational enclosure. Neither that expression nor 0.0594 is asserted to be the actual minimum set-to-set distance.

## 6. Interpretation carried into the paper

The hull strengthening matters because noncontact with a rim alone would not exclude passage through its interior. This theorem excludes the entire individual enclosing triangle and therefore any separately proposed aperture contained in it. It does not choose that triangle as a physical aperture or exclude arbitrary alternative spanning surfaces.

The model already has genuine reciprocal native/carrier work and a reversible receiving state. Its two distributed ports, host N contact cuts and diagnostic-ball cuts have distinct definitions. Empty G preimages prevent relabeling those definitions as direct G passage. Two input ports and six labels do not themselves prove that no future six-output map can exist.

This does not add a load, an ambient energy current, or a receiving-stock law. Local reversible exchange and later transfer elsewhere remain compatible aims.

The accepted central-route Q01 remains controlling: strict containment supplies an enclosing-cut gap, while inner source-free cuts can also have a positive delay when enclosing containment fails. A zero support distance is not a universal assertion of immediate nonzero field or flux. Conditional common-phase information claims and autonomous-F reconstruction limits also retain their original scope.

Historical source passages that predate reciprocal feedback, same-solution credit or completed exchange-only receiving/recovery remain historical. They do not become current missing results. Later receiving/recovery after F updates and preload remains a separate question.
