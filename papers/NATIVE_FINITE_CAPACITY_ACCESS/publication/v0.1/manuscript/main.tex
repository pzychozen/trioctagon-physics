\begin{abstract}
The joint projection ceiling of the reciprocal chiral exchange candidate limits the native trial storage that can leave the core, but a ceiling alone does not establish access to a definite fraction of that amount. We prove an explicit finite-time certificate for the coherent family $\Omega_0=(1,e^{i\phi},1)$, $0<\phi<\pi/12$, at fixed yaw $\pi/4$, with nonzero raw drive and initially empty carrier and receiver. The original equations retain finite positive propagation, finite-width periodic profiles and constant positive couplings. First fix admitted geometry and coefficients satisfying $\sigma_{\rm ext}\le\nu/2$, where $\nu^2=\eta_N^2mk^2R^2/(2\alpha_N)$ and $\sigma_{\rm ext}^2=c^2d/m+\kappa^2m/\beta_R$. Every phase in this family then satisfies, at the same time $t_*=1/\nu$,
\[
H_C(t_*)+H_R(t_*)>\frac{1053}{4096}C_{\rm joint}
>\frac14 C_{\rm joint},\qquad
C_{\rm joint}=\alpha_N(1-\cos\phi)>0.
\]
The proof retains the evolving launch drive in a Volterra comparison and bounds both propagation and receiver residuals of a strong reference curve. Exact rational estimates give a strict margin even at equality in the sufficient rate condition, whose finite positive regime is nonempty. The numerator is combined external stock and the denominator is joint capacity, not all initial native stock. The theorem establishes neither a receiver-only fraction nor subsequent recovery of this amount on these coefficients, saturation of the ceiling, or a spatial toroidal circuit.
\end{abstract}
