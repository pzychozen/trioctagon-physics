\section{Dimensions and normalized amplitudes}\label{sec:units}% SRC: I14 N06
The dimensional conventions follow P1 Section 1, Table 2 and P3 Appendix A, Table 1~\cite{p1,p3}. Let $\mathrm U$ denote carrier amplitude, $\mathrm L$ length, $\mathrm T$ exchange time and $\mathrm A_{\rm amp}$ native amplitude. The numerical launch identities in~\eqref{eq:drive0} use one native amplitude unit; restoring it attaches the corresponding amplitude powers to those identities. In particular, $R$ has an amplitude dimension even though its normalized value is $\sqrt3$.

Explicitly, the cancellations required by~\eqref{eq:rates} are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unitchecks}% SRC: I14 N06
\left[\frac{mk^2R^2}{\alpha_N}\right]=\mathrm T^{-2},
\qquad
\left[\frac{c^2d}{m}\right]=\mathrm T^{-2},
\qquad
\left[\frac{\kappa^2m}{\beta_R}\right]=\mathrm T^{-2}.
\end{equation}
Thus $B(u,\rho)$ is dimensionless, $A_0/\nu$ has the external-norm dimension, and the right side of~\eqref{eq:finitechoice} is dimensionless like $\eta_N^2$. Retaining $R^2$ is essential. These are commensurability checks, not physical calibration, native-frequency identification, an $F$-step cadence or evidence of a completed carrier circulation.
