\section{An explicit finite-time access certificate}\label{sec:theorem}
Define two positive estimate rates using the fixed registration, profiles and coefficients:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rates}% SRC: FA06 N02 N06
\nu^2=\frac{\eta_N^2mk^2R^2}{2\alpha_N},
\qquad
\sigma_{\rm ext}^2=\frac{c^2d}{m}+\frac{\kappa^2m}{\beta_R}.
\end{equation}
\begin{theorem}[Quarter-capacity access]\label{thm:access}% SRC: FA07 FA08 N05 N06
First fix admitted geometry and all finite positive coefficients of Section~\ref{sec:model}, at yaw $\pi/4$, satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:condition}% SRC: FA07 N05 N06
\sigma_{\rm ext}\le\frac{\nu}{2},\qquad t_*=\frac1\nu.
\end{equation}
For every $0<\phi<\pi/12$, the unique unchanged solution of~\eqref{eq:system} with the empty launch~\eqref{eq:launch} then satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:certificate}% SRC: FA08 I07 N05
\boxed{H_C(t_*)+H_R(t_*)>
\frac{1053}{4096}C_{\rm joint}>
\frac14 C_{\rm joint}>0.}
\end{equation}
The same coefficients and the same phase-independent time $t_*$ apply throughout this family. Equality in the sufficient rate condition is permitted.
\end{theorem}

The proof occupies Section~\ref{sec:proof}. Section~\ref{sec:regime} shows that the allowed finite positive regime is nonempty. The theorem concerns combined external stock at one specified time relative to the joint ceiling. The rates in~\eqref{eq:rates} are analytical estimate scales; neither identifies a physical native frequency or native-map cadence. No necessary or optimal threshold is asserted.
