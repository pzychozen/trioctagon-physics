\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
For the specified coherent empty-launch family at yaw $\pi/4$, one fixed finite positive coefficient choice satisfying $\sigma_{\rm ext}\le\nu/2$ guarantees combined external stock strictly exceeding $1053/4096$ of joint capacity at the phase-independent time $1/\nu$. The proof controls the actual evolving drive and both components of the external reference residual, with exact strict rational margins. The nonempty sufficient regime requires no change to the original equations or coefficients along a trajectory.

This advances the joint ceiling from a necessary bound to a definite finite-time access guarantee in the declared regime. Receiving and recovering that capacity-sized amount on the same coefficients, as well as the intended spatial route, remain subsequent questions. The accepted mathematics is preserved unchanged; the new manuscript and publication candidate await explicit manuscript-and-payload review.
