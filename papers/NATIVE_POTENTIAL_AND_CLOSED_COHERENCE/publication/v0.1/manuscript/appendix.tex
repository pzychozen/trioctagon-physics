\section{Finite-step pair-coherence identity}\label{app:pairidentity}

The following algebra fixes the sign and normalization independently of any descent assertion.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:pairidentity}
With tildes denoting prestage quantities and \(d_{ij}=\delta_j-\delta_i\),
\begin{align}
S_{ij}^+&=\widetilde S_{ij}\cos d_{ij}
-\widetilde A_{ij}\sin d_{ij},\label{eq:pairrotation}\\
\V(F\Omega)-\V(v)
&=-g\sum_{i<j}\left[
\widetilde S_{ij}(\cos d_{ij}-1)
-\widetilde A_{ij}\sin d_{ij}\right].
\label{eq:phasepotentialidentity}
\end{align}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By the convention \eqref{eq:pairs},
\[
\overline{F_i}F_j
=\overline v_i v_j e^{i(\delta_j-\delta_i)}
=(\widetilde S_{ij}+i\widetilde A_{ij})
(\cos d_{ij}+i\sin d_{ij}).
\]
Taking real parts proves \eqref{eq:pairrotation}. Synchronization preserves each intensity. Since \(|z_i-z_j|^2=|z_i|^2+|z_j|^2-2S_{ij}\), the edge-once coefficient \(g/2\) contributes \(-g\) times the coherence change, proving \eqref{eq:phasepotentialidentity}.
\end{proof}

The identity alone has no global sign. The local Hessian argument and the finite-domain pair-angle lemma supply their respective signs under different hypotheses.
