## Notation and scope reconciliation

The energy note's response matrix H, amplitude derivative A, phase form K, and eigenvectors v-plus/v-minus are named H_r, A_r, K_phi, and f-plus/f-minus in the manuscript. This separates response from complex coherence H, amplitude response from signed areas A_ij, the phase form from the scalar cycle factor, and local eigenvectors from the prestage v. These are notation changes only.

Section 8 (`sec:limits`) and Appendix B (`app:ownership`) retain the accepted scope: ideal mathematical recurrence, unchanged native zero convention, distinct 15-degree coherent sector, no physical-energy identification, no common-phase-history recovery, no moving support, no later geometry/SRG/control task. The representation comparison is a minimum over the stated raw-entry dictionaries for a joint energy-and-autonomy objective; it does not assert a coarsest sufficient energy quotient.

The archived `exact_algebra.py` and `exact_algebra_results.json` files in each evidence directory are supporting records from their original investigations. They were copied without execution. Continuous-domain theorems are supported by the written arguments and inherited hypotheses, not by an execution count.
