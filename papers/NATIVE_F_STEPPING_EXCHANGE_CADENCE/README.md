# Native Stepping and Exchange Cadence in the Reciprocal Chiral Exchange Candidate

Hilmir Frímann Halldórsson (pzychozen)

[Read the v0.1 edition](publication/v0.1/README.md) · [Publication status and review](publication/v0.1/PUBLICATION_STATUS.md)

Finite native-step accounts, pointwise post-native initial supply and receiving, local capacity, and qualified cadence limits. The accepted F01–F12 mathematics has passed manuscript review; the publication edition includes five small editorial correction groups and verified document records.

Later capacity-sized receiving and recovery under native stepping remains unproved. No physical cadence is selected. The edition records the accepted results and their limits together with editable sources and retained evidence.
