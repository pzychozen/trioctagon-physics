\begin{abstract}
The native Tri-Octagon state determines an intensity and a geometry-attached axial vector. We derive their sharp inequality and use them in an explicitly added mass--dipole dictionary. Newtonian gravity and magnetostatics are premises of this extension. For disjoint radial source profiles, harmonic averaging gives an exact two-body interaction. Native source updates alone do not balance its change: the same internal update produces different gravitational work at different separations and opposite magnetic work in parallel and transverse placements.

A proposed nonnegative store changes the advance along each frozen native update direction. Its midpoint rule conserves the specified pair-plus-store energy exactly and has a unique solution in stated nonzero load ranges. An initially dipole-silent Fourier preparation yields certified spatial response and neutral-placement predictions. Most directly, after one loaded step with gain \(s_{\rm on}\), a verified switch to zero load predicts the next gain \(2s_{\rm on}-1\), relative to a neutral copy at the same reached state. This test can reject the feedback without identifying a physical carrier. In this preparation, one calibrated channel amplitude determines the first gain; the full registered state is needed for the full vector test. We separate mathematical predictions, parameter identifiability and independent energy measurements. A physical store, instrument, clock and switching procedure remain to be identified.
\end{abstract}
