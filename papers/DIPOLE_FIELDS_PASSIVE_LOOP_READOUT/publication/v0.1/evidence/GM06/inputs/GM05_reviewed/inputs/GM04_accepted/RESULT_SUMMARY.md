# What GM04 establishes about magnetic field transfer

The existing circular carrier contains a real mathematical match to the magnetic dipole pattern, but the present equations do not complete magnetic transfer from one native triad to another.

**The field pattern works.** Prepare the circle with a steady nonzero tangent-encoded circulation. The curl of its exterior vector observation has the signed dipole angular pattern and inverse-cube decay. GM04 derives a bound valid in every direction, rather than relying on a few pictures or fitted slopes. The three allowed circle normals also match Paper G's three attachment directions exactly, with the correct ordering and reflection signs.

**The native source link is missing.** In C1 and RX, the mean circulation is conserved even while native ports drive the carrier. Starting with an empty circle cannot produce its circular-current moment. This remains true for the exact example in which one ordinary native update changes W from zero to nonzero. A preloaded circle can have a different moment with the very same native history. Separately, two states with equal intensity and opposite nonzero W can give exactly the same zero two-port input. Those ports alone do not determine W.

**The existing probe reads a different quantity.** On the loop axis, the published vector observation is exactly zero while its curl is nonzero. Every complete C6 frame therefore gives the same zero record for an empty carrier and this steady preload on the axis. Off-axis the vector observation can carry signed information, but no inspected equation takes its curl as the input to a second native triad.

The loaded WT extension needs its own qualification: incoming waves can change the circle's mean. With all carrier, line and receiver states initially empty, however, that mean stays zero before the first possible round trip. Later changes depend on actual return histories. WT's receiver lives on its six specified arcs, reads the tangent vector sector and is insensitive to the normal component of that sector. It does not supply a universal circulation-from-W rule or a curl-sensitive second triad.

Two specific additional choices would close the demonstrated gaps: a source law that actually creates the required carrier moment from the retained native information, and an observation/coupling law that makes a receiving triad respond to the curl. A three-circle realization would also need three explicit state laws and their source registration; geometric availability alone does not instantiate them. GM04 identifies these choices without adopting them.

The completed assessment is returned for review. It does not claim a physical current, SI calibration, electromagnetic propagation, force or material realization. No paper was drafted and nothing was published.
