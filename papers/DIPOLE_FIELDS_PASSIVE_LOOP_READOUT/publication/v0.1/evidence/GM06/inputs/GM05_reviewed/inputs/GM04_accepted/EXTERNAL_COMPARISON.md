# External dipole comparison

David Tong, *Electromagnetism*, section 3.3, equations (3.19)–(3.24), pages 54–57 of the printed notes (PDF pages 14–17).

https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/em/em2.pdf

Consulted 20 November 2026. This is the comparison for the dipole angular tensor and magnetic-moment convention only. GM04 derives its own loop curl, signs and uniform error bound. The source does not establish a Tri-Octagon carrier, native source rule, probe coupling or SI calibration. No full external PDF is redistributed in this package.
