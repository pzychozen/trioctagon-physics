Abstract
A steady tangent-encoded circulation on an adopted Tri-Octagon carrier circle produces a
vector observation whose spatial curl has the dipole angular tensor and inverse-cube leading
decay. We develop two signed tests from that exterior kernel. In the coaxial test the vector
observation vanishes at the receiving circle’s center, yet its oriented rim integral has the source
sign at every positive pair of radii and nonzero axial separation. Rotational symmetry makes
one ideal tangential rim sample suﬀicient in this special case.
The native map does not itself assign the required carrier means. A proposed three-loop
source impulse lifts the native planar observable 𝑊 to unique minimum-storage means under
equal weights. Its exact Fourier launch costs 6.768750025 × 10 −9𝐿𝛼2; conjugation reverses
the means at the same cost. A finite receiving circle on a registered rigid copy distinguishes
the two launches for every 𝑅 ≥ 100𝑤 , with a relative error certificate below one third. At
𝑅 = 100𝑤 , 1,024 additional exact contour samples also suﬀice. The circle center, body-center
probe, finite-area component, source offsets and complete delay window remain distinct. The
source impulse and contour readout are explicit additions. An internal source donor and a
law changing a second native triad remain open.
Status and provenance. This manuscript assembles the project-internally accepted GM04, corrected
GM05 and reviewed GM06 investigations. It incorporates the common-offset and latest-delay corrections.
After GPT review and the two wording corrections, the author approved repository publication. The tests
are mathematical consequences of specified equations; no external peer review or physical validation is
claimed. GPT supplied proposals and review; Codex supplied source verification, mathematical checks

not the radial magnetization profile for which the earlier paper obtained an exact external dipole
interaction.
