1 A silent center and a signed rim
The simplest test separates a point observation from a measurement around a loop. A steady
source circle and a coaxial receiving circle can have a zero vector observation at the receiving
circle’s center while the oriented rim integral is strictly signed. This is the first result to explain.
We then ask how a native state can prepare the source and whether the signed signal survives at
a receiver with the model’s actual finite radius.
The preceding GM01–GM03 paper imposed Newtonian and magnetostatic field laws through an
explicit source dictionary and studied interaction-driven feedback [4]. The present paper follows
a different chain: the adopted C6 exterior kernel, a prepared carrier, a proposed native-to-mean
impulse, and a passive contour readout [ 1, 2, 3]. A line circle has finite-radius corrections; it is
not the radial magnetization profile for which the earlier paper obtained an exact external dipole
interaction.
