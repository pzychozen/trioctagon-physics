For the original reciprocal exchange candidate, one finite positive coefficient tuple is fixed before every coherent empty launch with phase in (0, 1/100]. In the specified sufficient family, the paper connects inherited finite-capacity access to receiver storage above one quarter of joint capacity and later net native recovery above 24/25 relative to each stated intermediate external stock.

The normalization is joint capacity, not the whole initial native store. The receiver remains conservative; the result does not establish saturation, an exact nonlinear period, irreversible retention, monotonic recovery, a physical clock or a spatial route.
