# Exact accepted evidence

This directory preserves the accepted mathematical record rather than creating a new scientific evidence edition.

- accepted_investigation/ contains all 95 original Codex submission members, unchanged and in their internally consistent historical layout. The accepted proof, source ledger, crosswalk, exact-check records and selected P1–P4 sources are directly accessible there.
- NATIVE_CAPACITY_RECEIVING_RECOVERY_01_GPT_REVIEW.md is the actual subsequent scientific acceptance, copied exactly from the supplied review.
- NATIVE_CAPACITY_RECEIVING_RECOVERY_01_GPT_REVIEW_PACKAGE.zip and its original adjacent sidecar preserve the complete GPT review once at this edition level. The archive retains the original submission, original proposal packet, independent review records and exact replay at their historical scopes.

The expanded investigation and the original submission inside the review ZIP are intentional convenience/authority copies, verified byte-for-byte. No full earlier publication closeout or new outer manuscript-order ZIP is added.

Historical statements that the investigation was awaiting review remain unchanged. The later actual GPT review establishes acceptance of that investigation, not acceptance of this new manuscript. The fourteen exact-check groups and their independent historical replay remain fourteen groups; no scientific code was rerun for typesetting.

Current edition paths and original input origins are documented in ../SOURCE_IDENTITIES.json. The outer review package also carries INPUT_DELIVERY_MAPPING.json for all 112 original manuscript-packet members, mapped once to the candidate or review-support directories. Historical manifests keep their own roots; they are not silently rewritten to the new paths.
