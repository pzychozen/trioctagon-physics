In particular $B_1/\Lambda$, $B_2/\Lambda$, $C_1/D$ and $C_2/D$ are dimensionless. The structural projection account has storage units and adds no constitutive energy. These are commensurability checks, not physical calibration. The notation $L_{\rm err}$ is only an alias for the accepted error-constant sum $L=B_1+B_2$, used to distinguish it from carrier length $L_c$.
