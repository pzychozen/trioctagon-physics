\section{A nonempty finite positive parameter family}\label{sec:finite}
Fix any admitted finite geometry and positive finite $c,\alpha,\beta,G$. Then $k,m,d,R$ are finite and positive. Choose a finite
$\nu_{\rm target}\ge100c\sqrt{d/m}>0$, and set once, before phase,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:finitechoice}% RR34
 \eta=\frac{\nu_{\rm target}\sqrt{2\alpha}}{kR\sqrt m}>0,\qquad
 \kappa=\frac{\nu_{\rm target}}3\sqrt{\frac{\beta}{m}}>0 .
\end{equation}
Substitution yields $\nu=\nu_{\rm target}$, $\omega_R=\nu/3$ and
$0<\gamma/\nu\le1/100$. These coefficients remain constant on every trajectory. The construction involves no zero-speed actual system, infinite-coupling limit, profile change, receiver redesign, reset or added feedback.

The equality $\omega_R=\nu/3$ is material. This is an equality-constrained sufficient subfamily, not a proved open neighborhood of robust parameter values or a classification of all tuples allowed by P4. Appendix~\ref{sec:units} restores native amplitude units and verifies that both selected couplings are dimensionless.
