\section{Dimensions and restored native amplitude}\label{sec:units}
Use P1's abstract carrier units $\mathrm U,\mathrm L,\mathrm T$ and native amplitude unit $\mathrm A$~\cite{p1,p4}. The normalized numeric launch uses a unit amplitude $A_*$ with numeric value one. Restoring it gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:amplitude}% RR35
\begin{gathered}
 v=A_*(1,1,1),\quad \Omega_0=A_*(1,e^{i\phi},1),\quad R=\sqrt3\,A_*,\\
 D=K[v]_\times,\qquad |\ell|^2=\frac{20G^2A_*^2}{7},\\
 C=\alpha A_*^2(1-\cos\phi),\qquad
 X_0=-\sqrt\alpha\,A_*\sin\phi,\qquad y_0=2\sqrt C .
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
All initial deviations acquire the same $A_*$ factor; the directions $e,n$ stay dimensionless. Retaining $R^2$ in $\nu^2$ and the amplitude of $v$ in $D$ preserves the dimensionless identity $\omega_N^2/\nu^2=5/6$.

The coefficient $L$ is distinct from the length unit $\mathrm L$ and the circle length $L_c$. The dimensionally restored identity used in~\eqref{eq:theta} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dimtheta}% RR36
 \theta=\frac{\sqrt2\,A_*}{R}\sqrt{1-\cos\phi}\,\nu T
 \le\frac{\phi}{\sqrt3}\nu T<\frac1{25}.
\end{equation}
The factor $A_*/R=1/\sqrt3$ must not be suppressed in a dimensional identity. Both choices in~\eqref{eq:finitechoice} are dimensionless couplings. Restoring amplitude changes none of the normalized fractions or finite-error bounds. The reference frequencies are analytical quantities, not a physical clock or native-step cadence.
