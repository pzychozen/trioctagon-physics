The paper distinguishes a lossless labelled face representation from reduced observations. On the fixed positive SPR-01 one-update family, area and axial observations vanish while output intensity is globally injective. The existing symmetric pair matrix retains the signed coherence lost by population-only observation. The complete mathematical snapshot contains quadrature information beyond Hermitian coherence, with a sign-pair fibre except at the explicitly handled isotropic case.

Decoder invertibility, axial reconstruction, SPR-01 inverses and NRG's real-Gram fibre result are attributed to their foundations. New SGO-01 deductions, retained finite numerical evidence and open operational questions are identified separately. The negative RFO-02 result remains unchanged. No generic recurrence inverse, full-record dynamical closure, physical sensor, field identification, noise-robust reconstruction or computational advantage is established.
