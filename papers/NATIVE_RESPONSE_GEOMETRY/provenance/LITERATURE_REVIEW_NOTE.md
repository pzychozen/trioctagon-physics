Levin-Peres is the requested alternative to Kelly. The term **diagonal balance** denotes the stronger node-local condition; **SPD self-adjointness** denotes arbitrary positive-definite `GJ = J^T G`. In the nonnegative row-stochastic setting, positive diagonal balance expresses reversibility under the stated stationary/support hypotheses. Arbitrary SPD self-adjointness does not imply node-local detailed balance. The paper's existing restrictions outside that domain remain intact.
