## What was challenged and retained

The estimates were independently derived rather than accepted because they appeared in the order. The key vulnerabilities checked were the energy space at tent corners, the receiver-weight cancellation, a missing factor of two in core drift, the sign and derivative order of the propagation residual, a possible omitted product term in the power error, and improper interchange of small-time and large-coupling limits. The written argument resolves these points with the proposed constants unchanged.

All report equations R1–R30 are accounted for above. This is one sufficient fast-receiver regime. No attempt was made to lower the threshold, classify arbitrary couplings, establish exact receiver backflow at the witness, compute attainable capacity, insert native jumps or identify a spatial circuit.
