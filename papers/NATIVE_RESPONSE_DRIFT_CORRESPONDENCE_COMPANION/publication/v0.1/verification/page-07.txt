Remark 6.2 (What the neighborhood means) . The conclusion holds for every coeﬃcient direction
in a single suﬃciently small ball, including its equal-coeﬃcient lines. It is not restricted to
(−1, 0, 1). The radius is existential. The earlier ﬁnite D1 ﬁxture ladder does not supply a
numerical radius for this theorem. Other cancellation loci outside the ball, other branches and
larger rings remain outside the classiﬁcation.
The upper step endpoint is 1/4. The D1 border degeneracy at 1/3 is outside the domain and
cannot be crossed using this local argument. Nor do the positivity bounds certify attraction, a
global basin, or a state-selection mechanism.
