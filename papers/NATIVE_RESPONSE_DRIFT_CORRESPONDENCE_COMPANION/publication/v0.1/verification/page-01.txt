Abstract
Two established native-triad quantities contain an alternating factor in the channel co-
eﬃcients: the cycle-product obstruction of an in-phase response matrix and the common-
phase rate on a distinct chiral branch. We compare them at identical parameters, with
(ϵ, g, λ) = h(1, 1/6, 1/30) and mean-zero weak coeﬃcient heterogeneity. On one qualitative
neighborhood valid for every 0 ≤ h ≤ 1/4, the normalized response product and the se-
lected positive-label chiral rate factor by the same oriented coeﬃcient product with positive
symmetric real-analytic quotients. Their complete local zero sets and signs therefore agree;
the conjugate branch has the opposite rate. The quotient between these functions extends
through equal coeﬃcients, although only its leading value is explicitly evaluated. The re-
sponse product has nonzero leading order h3, whereas the per-update chiral advance has
order h, at ﬁxed suﬃciently small pairwise-distinct coeﬃcients. At h = 0 these statements
concern analytic generator extensions; the discrete map is the identity. A three-state compar-
ison explains the limit: the assigned in-phase diagnostic does not select the realized signed
advance without the actual state or branch. No attraction, physical-force law or calibrated
spatial motion is inferred.
1 Question and contribution
An alternating symmetry factor constrains two quantities without making them equal or causally
related. The earlier response study determines a diagonal-balance obstruction at positive aligned
states [ 1]. D0–D1 establish common-phase relative equilibria near twisted chiral references and
compute their cubic rate [ 2, 3]. The present question is whether these particular functions admit
a quantitative local correspondence at the same ordered coeﬃcients.
The accepted investigation RD01–RD05 answers that question [ 5, 6]. Its contribution is the
matched factorization, common local sign and zero theorem, normalized limit and precise state-
selection qualiﬁcation. The native map, positive branch, D1 branch and its rate coeﬃcient are
inherited. Full-state reconstruction on its admitted coherent domain is also prior work [ 4]; it is
not contradicted by comparing diﬀerent full states. The bounded source comparison supports
this project-level distinction, not a claim of worldwide priority.
