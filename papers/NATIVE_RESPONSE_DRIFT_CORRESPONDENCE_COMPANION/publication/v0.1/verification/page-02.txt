2 The native family and its three backgrounds

be proved to exist; no numerical radius is supplied.

A channel-mean phase gauge would fail at the twisted reference because its channel sum is zero.

These are diﬀerent states at the same parameters. The aligned and twisted reference orbits are

joining these backgrounds. At h = 0, F0 = id; only the divided equations below select particular
