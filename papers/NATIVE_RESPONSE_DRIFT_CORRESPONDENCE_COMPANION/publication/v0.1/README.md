A companion to *Native Response Geometry* and *Cubic Common-Phase Drift*. At identical parameters in the native triad, the normalized aligned response asymmetry and selected chiral common-phase rate share an alternating coefficient factor with positive analytic quotients on one qualitative local neighborhood. Their zero sets and signs agree with the stated branch convention; the exact analytic ratio extends through equal coefficients, while only its leading value is explicit. The raw response and actual per-update advance have distinct small-step normalizations.

The same assigned aligned diagnostic can accompany stationary or opposite chiral advances at different full states. State/branch selection therefore remains necessary. The paper supplies no numerical heterogeneity radius, attraction theorem, physical force/clock/route law, global phase predictor or AI-memory performance claim. Inherited response, D1 and reconstruction results retain their original ownership and domains.
