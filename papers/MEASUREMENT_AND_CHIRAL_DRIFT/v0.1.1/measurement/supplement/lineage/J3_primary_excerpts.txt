of two basis states, evolving unitarily until collapse by observation. In the Symbolic Resonance
Geometry (SRG) framework, we propose a generalized, symbolic analog: the symbolic qubit.
Unlike its quantum counterpart, the symbolic qubit does not depend on Hilbert spaces, unitary
evolution, or probabilistic collapse. Instead, it is composed of:
• A persistent symbolic identity — the Glyph ID
• A resonance amplitude R(t) — modulated recursively over time
• A symbolic memory curve H(t) — determining feedback sensitivity and coherence longevity
• Recursive evolution — updated via structured symbolic passes instead of continuous time
• Feedback coupling — symbolic entanglement formed via echo corridors, not physical super-
position
