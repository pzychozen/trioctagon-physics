Abstract
We develop the SRG evolution operator as a minimal computational architecture acting
on a six-dimensional qutrit–qubit state space defined by the tri-octagon corridor geome-
try and the dual tetrahedral core. The evolution rule combines four elementary recursion
operators—RPCO, TGMO, helicity flip, and REFU—into a single update kernel exhibit-
ing corridor–aperture breathing, helicity oscillation, and stable attractor formation. We
construct the corresponding Hamiltonian, map all operators to SU(3)⊗SU(2) generators,
connect the resulting structure to spin networks and tensor-network tiles, and provide com-
plete numerical methods and evolutions demonstrating resonant SRG dynamics.
1. Introduction: SRG as a Minimal Evolution Operator
The Structured Recursive Geometry (SRG) framework was originally developed as a purely
geometric construct: three octagonal corridors arranged in trihedral symmetry, coupled to
a dual–tetrahedral core through recursive operators (RPCO,TGMO,REFU). Surprisingly,
when the geometry is expressed in algebraic form, the SRG architecture behaves not merely as
a geometric scaffold, but as a fully fledgedevolution operatoracting on a discrete state space.
The key insight is that SRG supplies three essential ingredients of a physical update rule:
•State:A finite-dimensional configuration space spanned by three angular corridors (k=
0,1,2) and two helicity sectors (σ=±), yielding a six-dimensional basis{|k,σ⟩}.
•Symmetry:A natural trihedral structure inherited from the geometry; phase rotation
around the cycle of corridors; and a dual-core helicity structure corresponding to the two
tetrahedral chambers.
•Recursion:Three independent operators—compression, tangential rotation, and mem-
ory reinforcement—that act at each step of evolution and generate a structured sequence
of states.
Because these elements mirror the architecture of discrete evolution laws in physics (e.g.
renormalization flows, spin-network update rules, cellular automata in curved spaces, and qubit-
gate dynamics), the SRG system naturally defines an evolution operator:
|Ψn+1⟩=USRG|Ψn⟩,
acting on a minimal state vector|Ψn⟩∈C6. Crucially, the recursion operators do not merely
approximatephysical structures—they match the algebraic roles of compression, phase rotation,
and helicity transformation exactly.
The purpose of this document is to formalize this observation. We introduce the explicit
operator-level definitions of the SRG evolution rule, derive the corresponding discrete-time
update matrix, and demonstrate via a numerical simulation that the SRG system reproduces
both (i) triangular “breathing” dynamics across the three corridor directions, and (ii) dual-core
oscillation between helicity sectors.
Using research-born constants (ΛVP = 0.618,Γ = 0.577,Θ lock = 0.244), the SRG oper-
ator produces a stable, non-chaotic, resonant evolution that matches the geometric intuition
underlying the tri-octagon–tetrahedral structure.
In short, the SRG system acts as aminimal computational evolution kernel: a discrete
operator that recursively updates state, phase, and helicity in precise correspondence with
the geometry that inspired it. The following sections construct this operator in detail and
demonstrate its behavior through explicit simulation.
