A companion to [Spherical Edge Extensions of the Folded Tri-Octagon](../SPHERICAL_EDGE_EXTENSIONS/README.md). The complete static closed A-B classification has a unique interior noncollision transition at t=3h: all 42 vertices remain distinct while the interior facet counts change 56/44/50. Six local patches reorganize. No ten-vertex maximal facet occurs in this closed convex-envelope family; this does not exclude sections, projections, subsets or other enclosures.
