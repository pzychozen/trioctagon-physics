# Frozen method and resource ceiling — before scientific execution

Recorded 13 November 2026 after the DISTINCT_GAP source gate.

1. Reconstruct the exact folded geometry independently from Paper C's three maps and welding order. Compare all source vertices, material face cycles, lexicographic edges, midpoint/direction coefficients and endpoint label groups with the authenticated originals. Use `y_tilde=y/sqrt(3)` to reduce incidence algebra to Q(sqrt(2)); physical coordinates and the metric remain explicitly recoverable.
2. Nominate maximal facet label sets with existing SciPy/Qhull at three initial values only: 3/5, 2/3, and the proposed `(3sqrt(2)-3)/2`. Floating triangulations/orderings propose candidates only. No floating residual tolerance can validate a final certificate.
3. Generate each candidate plane by an exact triple cross product. For the two proposed open chambers, calculate every affine point residual polynomial, retain the complete identically zero set, and prove strict negativity of all other residuals throughout the open interval by exact polynomial root counting and a rational/algebraic sign test. Constant-event residuals are evaluated exactly. Check each polygon's directed edges against all other face vertices exactly, uniformly in the same domain.
4. Completeness uses a smaller certificate than enumerating all quadruples: every retained maximal polygon is an actual facet; every polygon side is an actual hull ridge; each such ridge has its two incident facets in the list. The facet adjacency graph of a full-dimensional convex polytope is connected, so this nonempty list closed under adjacency is all facets. This proves coverage at every parameter, not just at the nomination values. The proof will be given explicitly; Euler counts alone are not used for completeness.
5. If an exact support/order sign has an interior root, identify it exactly and refine only within A–B. Ceiling: at most 8 proposed open chambers, at most 64 new nominal evaluations and 32 extra bisection evaluations. No adaptive enlargement beyond the domain. If these finite resource limits do not finish the proof, return a precisely partial result.
6. Use the saved A/B faces as compatibility inputs; evaluate the new exit family and its limits against them. Do not rerun the old 816-triple enumeration. Compute the 12 inherited D3h actions by exact coefficient matching and derive face orbits from actual label permutations.
7. The independent checker reconstructs coordinates from folds, recomputes all support and cycle signs, complete zero sets, ridge adjacency, interval coverage, group action, candidate identity and endpoint mapping. It does not trust saved success flags. Generation and checking may share the disclosed Q(sqrt(2)) field/polynomial arithmetic; this is not two independent arithmetic implementations. SymPy is used only for parsing/presenting algebraic expressions if needed, SciPy only for nomination.
8. Negative controls: false transition or residual sign; omitted facet; incomplete zero set; broken branch/endpoint mapping; coverage gap. Mutated certificates are external test copies and never overwrite the accepted inputs or final candidate.

Each scientific worker is limited to **900 seconds wall time and 2 GiB process committed memory**. A Windows Job Object enforces the memory limit; an external supervisor enforces the timeout and preserves stdout/stderr/failure. No uncontrolled workers, dependency installations, repository writes or historical scientific replay are permitted. At most 8 nominal/refinement generation workers and 8 decisive checker/control workers are planned; implementation errors and their corrected reruns will be recorded rather than concealed.

The complete initial certificate uses at most 3 nominal hulls, 200 maximal-facet candidates across the three domains, 8,400 point residuals of degree at most three, and 8,000 directed polygon-edge tests of degree at most four. Additional exact finite work is limited to the 12 group actions, 42 branch identities, 52 inherited endpoint facets, and checker/negative-control validation. Shared repeated polynomial forms may be cached after exact normalization. These are ceilings, not claims of successful counts.
