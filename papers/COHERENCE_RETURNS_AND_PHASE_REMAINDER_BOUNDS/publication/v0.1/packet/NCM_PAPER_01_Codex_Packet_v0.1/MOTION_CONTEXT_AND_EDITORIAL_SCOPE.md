## What the new paper can honestly say

The series derives a regular coherence-return criterion and fixed-parameter constraints. At the investigated coefficients, the stated amplitude domain and regular three-update return hypotheses exclude mu=+i and mu=-i. The finite sixty-start search found no genuine three-stage candidate but does not prove nonexistence in the remaining domain. The paper should center these facts, distinguishing inherited results, the new deductions, and the independently reviewed sector strengthening.

This is not a claim that no native twelve-cycle exists under any circumstances, that the model has no motion, or that the author's geometric intuition has no value. It is also not affirmative evidence of native cone motion.
