The paper proves the regular raw-coherence return criterion and fixed-parameter amplitude, phase-sector and phase-remainder bounds. The closed 60-degree result concerns ordinary complex phases under the stated nonzero regular finite-return hypotheses in B3, not a spatial cone angle or global convergence. The finite 60-start search remains inconclusive for other dispersed-phase returns. No native-to-cone motion correspondence or physical energy law is established.
