## Title and purpose

**Certified Connection from Local Period-Doubling to the Captured Alternating Cycle in the Native Triad**

*A companion to Geometry-Attached Axial Observables, Period-Doubling, and Certified Entrance Capture in the Tri-Octagon Map.*

The reader should leave knowing why the local M2 cycle and the separately captured M3 cycle now belong to one certified branch, and exactly why this does not establish attraction over the whole branch. Prefer roughly 10–14 readable pages; the complete argument, not a page quota, controls the final length.

## Abstract ingredients

State the previous separation of onset and endpoint; unchanged exact Paper G family; the removable onset formulation; uniform first patch anchored at zero; full continuation through 128 patches and 127 common-uniqueness joins; exact endpoint inclusion in the inherited trap; primitive full-state two-cycles; and the distinct attraction strip 4999/1000000 <= mu <= 1/200. End with the unresolved full-range attraction and mathematical-not-physical scope. Do not call the whole family attracting.

## Proposed organization

### 1. The missing connection

About half a page. Explain the old M2/M3 distinction and the new logical implication. Preserve Paper G as an earlier correct edition. A small inherited/new/open table prevents rebranding earlier discoveries. Cite bounded prior-work comparison without a worldwide priority claim.

### 2. Exact family, quotient and inherited premises

About 1–2 pages. Give the native recurrence/order, exact coefficients, host selection, basis, critical value and positive gauge. Define z=(xi,eta), H and the symmetry relation. State inherited M2 local uniqueness/attraction and M3 endpoint uniqueness/capture with the minimum hypotheses required later. Do not reproduce the full old host, cubic-coefficient or capture proofs. Give a concise notation crosswalk.

### 3. A removable formulation anchored at onset

About 2–3 pages. Show t=mu/nu, a=sqrt(t), xi=tX and eta=a(e+t y e_perp). Explain exclusion of the trivial host at positive mu. Give the normalized residual, original prestage/phase/gauge correspondence, exact divided differences and entire-function tails. Include the limiting equations and full block Jacobian. Explain the actual M2 germ identification and the first interval reaching mu=0. Detailed algebra may sit in a compact appendix, not disappear behind “the computer verifies.”

### 4. Uniform continuation and identification at joins

About 2 pages. State the parameter-uniform contraction lemma, preconditioned center estimate, exact rational boxes and regularity margins. Explain why 128 intervals cover the parameter set and why 127 convex common-uniqueness regions identify adjacent roots. Report root-solver bounds as root-solver bounds. A compact table supplies key accepted constants and evidence locations.

### 5. The M3 endpoint and full-state cycles

About 1 page. Refine inside the final patch, reconstruct the entire endpoint enclosure into the old quotient chart, and use strict inclusion in exact U_plus plus inherited uniqueness. Explain positive parity and why Um is not used. State primitive quotient and full-state period two for positive mu, with inherited symmetry proof. Endpoint capture now lands on this branch, but entrance at other parameters is not established.

### 6. Separate, limited attraction result

About 1 page. Differentiate the actual map, not the root solver. Present P=(H D Fhat)^2, the inverse-defect norm argument and the exact strip. Record M2 local attraction with unspecified upper extent, the new explicit strip and the M3 trap separately. The middle range remains unresolved; failed sufficient bounds are not losses of stability. State common-phase neutrality.

### 7. Evidence, reproducibility and limits

About 1 page plus references. Explain shared-engine replay, separately organized exact comparisons, 31 supporting checks, four rejection controls and prior Linux diagnostic scope without inflated independence claims. Distinguish this manuscript's document-only execution from earlier scientific runs. Give relative evidence links, exact source identities and the pending manuscript-review status. Conclude with the new connection—not a demand for another stability investigation.

### Optional compact appendix

Use only for exact removable algebra, derivative tails or a proof-critical notation reconciliation that would otherwise disrupt the body. No long dump of all patch endpoint fractions. Full machine-readable certificate remains in the evidence tree.

## Recommended result structure

- One main connection theorem at the exact fixed family, with mu=0 as limiting anchor and 0<mu<=1/200 as the nontrivial interval.
- Supporting removable-system lemma and uniform patch/join argument.
- Full-state period-two corollary, explicitly an application of inherited symmetry.
- Separate attraction proposition on the explicit strip only.

Avoid counting I01/I02 anew or splitting each file check into a theorem.

## Tables and optional figure

A table should distinguish the connection interval, the first patch, total patch/join counts, root validation norms, regularity bounds, endpoint inclusion margin and actual dynamical bound. Mark exact rational quantities versus coarsened accepted bounds. Read source values from saved records.

An optional proof-dependency/interval diagram can show M2 germ -> first patch -> joins -> M3 trap. Stability belongs on a different track. Do not assign a numerical length to M2's qualitative stable neighborhood. Use an inset or broken scale for the tiny explicit endpoint strip and label that device. A schematic does not portray a material trajectory, a parameter ramp or a sampled proof.

## Final editorial tests

The abstract must not promise a stable branch everywhere. The theorem must not include a nontrivial two-cycle at mu=0. The onset argument must not start after an unproved numerical gap. The joins must not rely on mere overlap. The endpoint must use all of its enclosure, not its center. The full-state conclusion must retain regularity and phase symmetry. The conclusion must leave full-range attraction unresolved while stating that the branch-connection question is closed.
