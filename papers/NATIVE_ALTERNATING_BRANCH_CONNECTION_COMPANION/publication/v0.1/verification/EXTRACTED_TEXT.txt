Abstract
Paper G established a local supercritical period-doubling branch and, separately, capture
by an alternating cycle at a speciﬁed ﬁnite parameter. We explain the accepted connection
between those particular objects for the unchanged exact native-triad family. An amplitude
formulation removes the apparent singularity at onset and ﬁxes a nontrivial phase orientation.
Its ﬁrst uniform patch includes the bifurcation parameter. A ﬁnite certiﬁcate then covers

endpoint enclosure lies strictly inside the exact inherited M3 trap, identifying the continued
root with its unique two-step ﬁxed point. The branch is a graph over µ, continuous to
the host and smooth for positive parameters. Inherited symmetry lifts every member to a
primitive full-state two-cycle. A separate derivative bound proves local attraction modulo
common phase only on 4999/1000000 ≤ µ ≤ 1/200. Attraction throughout the connected
interval remains unresolved. These are mathematical statements about a ﬁxed discrete map,
with no physical-ﬁeld identiﬁcation or parameter ramp imposed on an orbit.

a bounded comparison within the inspected project corpus, not a worldwide priority claim.
