# Tri-Octagon: Geometric Origins, Normalization, and Constant Interpretations

**Hilmir Frímann Halldórsson — historical companion v0.2, 19 October 2026.**

[Read the twelve-page PDF](GEO_ORIGINS01_v0.2.pdf) · [Editable TeX](GEO_ORIGINS01_v0.2.tex) · [Bibliography](references.tex) · [Build guide](BUILD.md) · [Sources and access](ACCESS_AND_EVIDENCE_MAP.md) · [Edition notes](EDITION_NOTES.md).

This expanded historical edition explains the author's remembered route from conversations about the universe, symbolism and a visual association with the vesica piscis to the geometric questions he pursued. His tentative hierarchy of forms, the roles he assigned to π, e and φ, changing perspectives, and the precise half-π overlap result are presented with their stated uncertainties.

The mathematical account remains intact: eight numbered sections, twenty-three equations with their original numbers, the existing event table, and both existing figure families. The opening distinguishes recollection and symbolic motivation from exact geometry and conditional physical proposals. It introduces no new physics result or experiment.
